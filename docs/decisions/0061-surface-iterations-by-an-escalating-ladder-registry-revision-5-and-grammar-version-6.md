# 0061. Surface iterations by an escalating ladder, registry revision 5's region, and grammar version 6

Date: 2026-09-11. Status: Accepted. Source: the owner's instruction of 2026-09-11 that the explorable-planet surface level is the next randomization level, that its iterations start with shorter and simpler cycles and grow in complexity with each next one, and his adoption of the seven-rung ladder below. Supersedes the order-of-the-rest clause of [decision 0047](0047-usable-storage-and-structure-generation-then-solar-system-iterations.md), and carries the registry revision and grammar version its first cycle needs in the same record, on the precedent of [decision 0055](0055-definition-level-tags-and-tagged-references.md). Settles, for the surface level only, the three parts of [review finding 48](../review.md#48-every-randomization-level-is-to-be-judged-graphically-and-nothing-says-which-view-in-what-order-or-against-what-vector) that stayed open after decisions 0052 and 0055.

## Context

The solar-system level's loop is closed: four iterations ran and the owner accepted iterations 3 and 4 ([progress](../progress.md#solar-system-iterations)). The next level down is the explorable planet's surface, and [decision 0047](0047-usable-storage-and-structure-generation-then-solar-system-iterations.md)'s "Order of the rest" does not contain it: that clause puts the content-gate test plan of [decision 0002](0002-primitive-set-content-gate.md), the minimal Godot viewer of M0a exit criterion 11, and the content gate after its step two, and nothing else. Beginning surface iterations therefore reorders an accepted record, which takes a record of the same shape decision 0047 has — an owner instruction, written down.

What judges a surface iteration is unrecorded, and that is not an oversight but a finding: [finding 48](../review.md#48-every-randomization-level-is-to-be-judged-graphically-and-nothing-says-which-view-in-what-order-or-against-what-vector), raised from the owner's own statement that every randomization level is eventually judged graphically, holds that nothing says which view, in what order, or against what vector, for any level. Two of its four parts are answered: [decision 0052](0052-the-two-levers-compose-a-destination-and-a-review-view-shows-it.md) made the system view a review harness rather than a core-release feature, so the close-views clause of [decision 0045](0045-close-views-only-in-the-surface-sky-and-a-derived-system-description.md) stands, and [decision 0055](0055-definition-level-tags-and-tagged-references.md) supplied the content prerequisite for the solar-system level. Three stay open — the order of the levels, the view each of the other two is judged in, and what a graphic judgement records as its vector — and they are the owner's.

The code cannot compose a surface at all. No registry revision declares a `region` category; revision 4's nine are `star`, `barycentre`, `planet`, `atmosphere`, `surface-material`, `texture-recipe`, `geometry-recipe`, `artifact-part`, and `artifact`. No grammar roots or produces the `surface` or `planet` domain: every version's roots are the solar-system and artifact domains, so `compose --domain surface` fails with "Grammar version 4 roots no graph of domain 'surface'". `TransformKind.SurfaceAnchor` and its four components — latitude, longitude, height, heading — are encodable and unused by every production. There is no heightfield, terrain mesh, chunk, or region-field generator, and nothing reads a stored planet node and produces surface geometry: the whole of "region" in the core is the one number of [decision 0059](0059-region-limits-are-a-pinned-record.md). What exists and is reusable is the content-to-engine adapter of [decision 0055](0055-definition-level-tags-and-tagged-references.md), the raster rule of [decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md), and the harness shape of [decision 0052](0052-the-two-levers-compose-a-destination-and-a-review-view-shows-it.md) and [decision 0054](0054-the-review-panel-carries-the-summary-while-the-system-is-framed.md).

What the level may not do is settled before it starts, by arithmetic over constants the decisions already fix ([primitives part 5](../primitives.md#the-arithmetic-that-decides-it)): a minimum-radius landable body is some 823,500 maximal regions and an Earth-sized one some 121.6 million, so materialising the smallest costs 804 GiB against the 300 MiB a 300-region campaign budgets, and 121.6 million against a 65,536-instance ceiling is a factor of 1,855. Regeneration is mandatory for terrain, and a planet is not one graph. Neither figure is an estimate, so neither waits on the M0b measurement.

## Decision

Each clause below stands alone, so the owner could accept or amend one at a time; he accepted all of them as drafted on 2026-09-11. A clause marked **open** was a choice this record put to him; a clause marked **follows** applies an accepted record and was open only in the sense that anything is.

**1. The order of the levels (open).** The surface level's iterations come next, the artifact level after them, and the content-gate test plan of [decision 0002](0002-primitive-set-content-gate.md) and the content gate after those. M0a exit criterion 16 therefore moves further out, and the compatibility cost decisions [0030](0030-m0a-criterion-1-proceeds-before-the-p0-exit-record.md) and [0047](0047-usable-storage-and-structure-generation-then-solar-system-iterations.md) already accepted extends to this overlap: every set, graph, and destination minted before the gate may be superseded. Requirement R6 holds throughout — every structure composes from stored sets.

**2. The ladder, named in advance (open; adopted by the owner on 2026-09-11).** A surface iteration adds exactly one capability, so a row records what it added rather than deciding its scope on the turn. The rungs, in order, with what already specifies each and what each still needs:

**3. Cycle one is rung 1 and nothing more (open).** It generates, per lever seed at the starter tier, one `region` node under the destination's landing-candidate planet, attached by a `SurfaceAnchor` transform whose latitude, longitude, height, and heading the grammar draws, carrying one parameter, and wearing that planet's own stored `surface-material` rastered by `derive-surface-raster/1` ([decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md)). No relief, no biome, no scatter, no far field, no sky, no chunks: each is a later rung, and scaffolding one early is what [decision 0002](0002-primitive-set-content-gate.md) and [decision 0024](0024-tests-comments-index-and-scaffolding-trimmed.md) refuse.

What that earns a verdict on is one question no existing picture asks, and it has a number before anything is rendered. `texture-recipe.scale` is a real metre length from registry revision 4 — from 0.99 mm to 4 m, default 1 m ([decision 0060](0060-registry-revision-4-units-defaults-and-validation-limits.md)) — so a 128-pixel tile laid at its stored scale repeats 2,048 times across a maximal region, 6.25 cm to a texel. Resolution is therefore not the fault to look for; **periodicity is**. The body view cannot ask it, because it clamps the repeat to between one and eight where a body is drawn far from its own size, and iteration 4's reading already needed close-ups because a material cannot be judged on a body a few pixels across ([progress](../progress.md#solar-system-iterations)). Cycle one is that close-up taken to its end, and a failure there sends the recipe's scale range or `derive-surface-raster/2` back before relief is built on them.

**4. The region is a node in the system graph (open).** Its instance path is `solar-system/planet/N/region/0`, which is the shape `StreamPath`'s own documented example assumes. Graph-to-graph composition is deferred to rung 7, where a planet carries many regions or chunks and the arithmetic of [primitives part 5](../primitives.md#the-arithmetic-that-decides-it) forbids one graph; until then a specification naming one set and one domain is enough, and re-minting the graphs at that rung costs nothing because iteration output is disposable ([decision 0047](0047-usable-storage-and-structure-generation-then-solar-system-iterations.md)). The alternative, a surface graph of its own naming its parent graph and instance path as `composition-graph-specification/2`, is the right answer at rung 7 and premature now.

**5. `extent` is a parameter of the region category (open).** One parameter, in metres, with [decision 0017](0017-region-extent-cap-and-storage-derivation.md)'s 2,048 m as its maximum and its default. From registry revision 4 every parameter carries its unit, its default, and the decoder's bounds inside the record ([decision 0060](0060-registry-revision-4-units-defaults-and-validation-limits.md)), so an extent held there meets the standard [finding 50](../review.md#50-a-stored-quantity-carries-no-unit-no-default-and-no-validation-limit) set, and the category does not start life empty, which is the condition [finding 53](../review.md#53-a-barycentre-holds-nothing-so-a-published-pair-contradicts-its-own-masses) shows to be expensive.

It is deliberately **not** a second version of the region limits. [Decision 0059](0059-region-limits-are-a-pinned-record.md) grows that record with the region rules [decision 0044](0044-tiered-system-composition-one-star-and-explorability-by-validation.md) defers, which are per-tier *counts*; an extent is a units-and-bounds constant of the kind [decision 0010](0010-units-coordinates-and-region-bounds.md) tabulates. And the cost of putting it there is disproportionate: the description prints the region limits' version and hash, so a version 2 would move the description's version, re-record its frozen fixed-seed vector a second time, and change every destination's pack identifier — for a number that is neither what a suit survives nor what a body's shape allows, which is the distinction decision 0059 exists to keep.

**6. Registry revision 5 and grammar version 6 (open as to their content; follows as to their form).** Revision 5 is revision 4 with one added category, `region`, admitted to the solar-system and surface domains and carrying the parameter of clause 5. Grammar version 6 lays out version 3's rules over revision 5 and adds one production: a surface-anchor connector on `planet` of cardinality 0 to 1, admitted only where region limits version 1 admits the body — which is the first time decision 0041's clause that the grammar admits a surface anchor only above the minimum landable radius is honoured by code rather than by a description. Revisions 1 to 4 and grammar versions 1 to 5 stay supported, so every earlier iteration reproduces from its record ([decision 0050](0050-registry-revision-2-grammar-version-2-and-versioned-record-growth.md)). The vocabulary is authored for the newest revision, as that decision requires, with one `region` template; its hash moves and re-mints the set pack and every destination, which is free because iteration content is disposable and is not a kept destination ([decision 0047](0047-usable-storage-and-structure-generation-then-solar-system-iterations.md)).

Two consequences of the form, not of the choice. A region cannot be hung on the published revision 2 and 3 content, because a grammar binds to a registry revision and a definition's bytes are laid out under the revision its label names ([decision 0035](0035-category-registry-record-and-generator-revision-identifiers.md), [decision 0055](0055-definition-level-tags-and-tagged-references.md)); cycle one therefore publishes a fresh set and fresh destinations. And registry revision 4 will have carried no published content at all, having been overtaken before a vocabulary was authored for it.

**7. The view: two review-harness frames (open as to the frames; follows as to their status).** A surface iteration is read from two pictures per seed, taken in one run: a **walk frame** at 2 m eye height at the region centre looking along the region's stored heading, and a **top-down orthographic frame** of the whole region with a scale bar. The eye height is 2 m because that is the height decision 0041 derives the 524,288 m minimum from, so the picture and the constant rest on one assumption; the orthographic frame exists because a flat 2,048 m plane is illegible from inside it and a reader must see the extent and the repeat.

Both are review-harness frames, not core-release views, so the close-views clause of [decision 0045](0045-close-views-only-in-the-surface-sky-and-a-derived-system-description.md) stands exactly as [decision 0052](0052-the-two-levers-compose-a-destination-and-a-review-view-shows-it.md) left it: the core release still adds no approach scene, telescope, scanner zoom, or orbital view. A player-facing surface view would reverse that clause and is not proposed here.

What the frames supply rather than read is stated on screen, as the legend of [decision 0057](0057-the-view-draws-what-the-content-stores.md) states its remaps. In cycle one that is at least the light direction, because the celestial solution of [decision 0032](0032-astronomically-consistent-surface-sky.md) is unbuilt and stands at Not started ([progress](../progress.md#verification-and-performance-targets)); a surface read under an invented light without saying so is decision 0057's fault repeated one level down. Conversely, where the body view had to clamp the pattern's repeat because a body is drawn far from its own size, a region drawn at true scale can lay the stored scale honestly, and by decision 0057's principle it must.

**8. The sample: six seeds for cycle one, twenty-four from rung 2 (open).** Cycle one reads six of the starter tier's twenty-four lever seeds, named in its row so cycle two compares like with like; the sample returns to the full twenty-four at rung 2, where relief first makes one region differ from another. Twenty-four near-identical flat planes would cost the owner's reading and buy nothing, which is what "shorter and simpler first" means in practice.

**9. Where a graphic sample is kept (open).** Outside the repository, at `build/samples/surface<n>-starter/` on the workstation, cycle one's being `build/samples/surface1-starter/` — the practice the solar-system rows already follow, recorded rather than assumed, which is the fourth part of [finding 48](../review.md#48-every-randomization-level-is-to-be-judged-graphically-and-nothing-says-which-view-in-what-order-or-against-what-vector).

**10. The vector is on the inputs (follows).** An image is not bit-identical across drivers and renderers, so image bytes are evidence for a human verdict and never a determinism vector — decision 0047's requirement that an iteration reproduce from its record is met by pinning what produced the pictures. A surface row pins: the registry revision and hash; the grammar version and hash; the vocabulary hash; the set pack; the destination pack and graph hash per seed; the lever seed list; `derive-surface-raster`'s version, which [decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md) already requires of any row read from pictures; the description version; and the suit profile, tier profile, and region limits with their hashes ([decision 0049](0049-suit-profile-and-system-description-version-1-and-derivation-rules-as-named-implementations.md), [decision 0058](0058-tier-rules-are-a-pinned-record.md), [decision 0059](0059-region-limits-are-a-pinned-record.md)). A raster sheet is not among the frames of clause 7 because the raster already carries a literal digest compared on both operating systems, so a sheet would add no evidence the suite does not hold.

**11. The verdict, and the numbers beside it (follows).** Mechanically first, as every iteration is judged: every landing candidate carries exactly one region, every anchor sits on a body region limits version 1 admits, every extent is within decision 0017's cap, and two processes compose the graph byte for byte. The row also prints two counts — regions per destination, and distinct surface materials across the sample — which is the cheap first step [finding 55](../review.md#55-uniqueness-is-unguarded-above-the-artifact) recommends: make repetition visible rather than corrected, so a verdict rests on a number as well as on pictures. Then the owner reads the sample and records plausible-and-varied or not, with what the next cycle changes, which the ladder has already named. Every row lives in [progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations), a sibling of the solar-system table, and reproduces from its pins.

**12. The landing-candidate flag stays provisional and the description stays at version 3 (open; this is a reading of decision 0047 that the owner may amend).** [Decision 0047](0047-usable-storage-and-structure-generation-then-solar-system-iterations.md) replaces the radius-and-suit flag with decision 0044's validation outcome "once regions are generated, a version change of the description under decision 0045". Cycle one generates regions without validating them, and the reading proposed here is that the trigger is validation rather than existence: the flag becomes a validation outcome at the rung where a region can pass or fail the suit profile, and until then the description's wording, its version 3, and its frozen vector are untouched ([decision 0059](0059-region-limits-are-a-pinned-record.md)). The alternative reading — that generating a region fires the trigger now — would make cycle one owe a description version and a re-recorded vector for a flag whose meaning had not changed, which is the cost decision 0049 asks to be paid only for a real improvement.

**13. The owner's three generation phases are a grouping over the lifecycle's six steps (open).** The owner states that travel to a new solar system has three phases: solar-system generation; explorable-planet surface generation and everything on it; artifact generation, each artifact unique, and artifact placement. No accepted record states that shape — the six numbered steps of the [generation lifecycle](../technical-design.md#generation-lifecycle) are the only recorded phasing, and the closest thing to the three is the raising note of [finding 48](../review.md#48-every-randomization-level-is-to-be-judged-graphically-and-nothing-says-which-view-in-what-order-or-against-what-vector), which records the owner's own statement of three randomization *levels* and is open.

The phases are therefore recorded as a grouping and the steps are **not renumbered**: phase 1 is the system half of step 2, phase 2 is its planet half together with step 3 less artifacts, phase 3 is step 3's artifacts and their placement, and steps 1, 4, 5, and 6 stay lifecycle-wide. Renumbering would break a citation in an accepted record, because [decision 0044](0044-tiered-system-composition-one-star-and-explorability-by-validation.md) makes explorability turn on "step 4 of the generation lifecycle" by number. The checkpoints of step 6 fall at least at the phase boundaries, which are the "safe boundaries" that step already asks for, and finer within a phase once chunks exist, because a whole-phase checkpoint would re-run an interrupted surface phase entire.

Two things this clause does not settle are named in the next section: the per-phase retry and refund rule, and decision 0004's effort score. And one thing it settles by citation rather than by choice: the owner's requirement that each artifact be unique is delivered for the artifact object within a campaign by [decision 0007](0007-artifact-collision-substitution-and-campaign-salt.md), whose scope [concept 6](../concept.md#6-the-artifacts) already bounds to the campaign; what decision 0007 does not compare is *placement*, which is not in an appearance recipe though [concept 6](../concept.md#6-the-artifacts) counts arrangement and environmental context as part of recognizability. Placement is therefore a uniqueness scope no record covers, which is [finding 55](../review.md#55-uniqueness-is-unguarded-above-the-artifact) one level down, and it belongs to the artifact series and not to this ladder.

**14. Each phase depends on the previous one, and a dependency is a pin (open as to the rule; follows as to its shape).** The owner states that phase 2 takes phase 1's output as its input and phase 3 takes phase 2's. That is recorded as an identity rather than as an ordering convention: a later phase names the earlier phase's output by its content hash, so the dependency survives being run out of order, on another machine, or years later, which is what the whole content-addressed store exists for. Nothing may rest on the sequence a generator happens to run in.

**Cycle one satisfies the dependency by construction, which is an argument for clause 4's deferral rather than against it.** A region that is a node of the system graph inherits that graph's identity for free: the graph record's hash covers the region, the destination already pins one graph by pack and hash, and there is nothing a second pin could add. A separate surface graph would have to acquire the pin deliberately, because a graph specification names a registry revision, a generator, a grammar, a seed, one set, and one domain, and no parent graph — so rung 7 does not merely add a field, it takes on the obligation of recording a dependency that is currently free. That is the second reason to defer it, beside the arithmetic of clause 4.

**The stream derives down the same chain, by the path and never by the parent's stream.** A composition draws each instance from the specification's seed and that instance's own path ([decision 0021](0021-sha256-stream-derivation.md), [decision 0019](0019-generator-version-1-frozen.md)). Cycle one's anchor draw — the region's latitude, longitude, height, and heading — therefore derives from the composition seed and `solar-system/planet/N/region/0`, whose prefix is the planet node's own path, so the dependency and the determinism have one shape and neither needs a mechanism of its own. What it must not do is consume the planet node's stream: [decision 0031](0031-primitive-complete-composition-and-storage.md) states that one chunk never consumes another's random stream, and [decision 0041](0041-planet-fields-tangent-regions-minimum-radius-and-far-field.md) admits the planet's fields to a region as parent-to-child *data* flow for the same reason. The distinction is exact and is easy to state wrongly: the parent's path is inherited, the parent's stream is not. A surface graph of its own at rung 7 loses the inherited prefix and must re-establish the chain deliberately, by deriving its seed from the parent graph's hash and the planet's instance path.

**Retry runs downstream; invalidation runs upstream.** A later phase retries itself against its predecessor's stored output, which is sound today precisely because a destination is already a stored record naming its graph by pack and hash ([decision 0053](0053-a-destination-is-a-stored-record-addressed-by-its-levers.md)): the surface phase re-draws against a system that is on disk rather than against one it must re-compose. The converse is the expensive half and holds just as strictly: redrawing an earlier phase invalidates every later one, because every later identity is derived from the earlier output's hash. Per-phase attempt budgets, the rule of which phase a failure re-draws, and the single destination-wide refund are named in the next section as what this record does not settle.

**Checkpoints and caches follow the same chain.** Phase boundaries are the natural resume points of step 6 of the [generation lifecycle](../technical-design.md#generation-lifecycle), as clause 13 states, and an upstream phase's identity belongs in a downstream cache key: [decision 0031](0031-primitive-complete-composition-and-storage.md) already requires a cache key to include world identity, exact primitive revisions, parameters, generator and grammar versions, chunk identity, and level of detail, and an upstream graph's hash is the world identity at the level below it. Cycle one builds no cache, so this clause binds nothing yet and is stated here so rung 2 does not have to rediscover it.

**15. The artifact level is a series of its own (open).** The ladder ends at rung 7. Artifact generation and placement are a separate numbered series with its own table in [progress](../progress.md#milestones), because [finding 48](../review.md#48-every-randomization-level-is-to-be-judged-graphically-and-nothing-says-which-view-in-what-order-or-against-what-vector) already treats the levels as things to be ordered rather than nested; because the artifact domain is the one domain that composes today, its parts already carrying geometry and material references, so its series starts from a view rather than from a registry revision; and because uniqueness is an artifact concern with no counterpart on this ladder. Each series' verdict lives in its own section of [progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations), which stays the single status source ([decision 0011](0011-requirements-single-source.md)).

## Consequences

**A known cost, chosen deliberately.** The owner took the ladder rather than settling [finding 54](../review.md#54-the-registry-the-grammars-and-the-numeric-tables-are-code-where-the-vocabulary-is-content) first, so the registry, the grammars, and the numeric tables stay C# where the vocabulary is data, and rungs 2, 3, 4, and 7 each fire that finding's trigger again: a rung is a rebuild where a vocabulary change is a file edit and a rerun. Finding 54 stays **open** and this record clears nothing of it; it records the cost so the next reader knows it was counted rather than missed.
