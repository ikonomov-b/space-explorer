# 0055. A definition carries tags of its own, a reference may require them, and registry revision 3 gives a planet its surface

Date: 2026-09-10. Status: Accepted. Source: the owner's choice of 2026-09-10 among three ways for a leaf primitive to declare what it suits. Applies the versioned-record growth of [decision 0050](0050-registry-revision-2-grammar-version-2-and-versioned-record-growth.md) a second time and extends the connector-tag rule of [decision 0048](0048-composition-grammar-version-1-graph-records-and-graph-publication.md) from a connector to a definition and to a reference parameter. It clears no review finding: it supplies the appearance content that the third part of [review finding 48](../review.md#48-every-randomization-level-is-to-be-judged-graphically-and-nothing-says-which-view-in-what-order-or-against-what-vector) names as a prerequisite, and settles nothing else that finding asks.

## Context

A solar system's bodies have no appearance in the database. `planet` carries no material reference under either registry revision, and `surface-material` and `texture-recipe` are admitted to the surface and artifact domains only ([primitives](../primitives.md#part-1-the-primitive)), so the review view of [decision 0052](0052-the-two-levers-compose-a-destination-and-a-review-view-shows-it.md) draws stand-in spheres coloured by body type — a look chosen in the viewer. The concept of record admits no such thing: materials, textures, geometry, collision, physical properties and behaviour are primitive concerns rather than engine-owned facts, and no separate environment description bypasses the primitive composition ([concept 8](../concept.md#8-randomness-from-expandable-primitives)). The primitive-completeness invariant says the same normatively: every rendered property originates in an exact primitive revision, its instance parameters, or an overlay ([technical design](../technical-design.md#primitive-registry-and-composition)).

Giving `planet` a plain reference to a `surface-material` is not enough. The generator fills a reference from the definitions of the referenced category generated so far, by category alone ([decision 0042](0042-category-registry-revision-1-first-content-records-generator-and-publish-protocol.md)), so an icy body would draw a metallic-green material as readily as an ice one, and a body's look would be unrelated to the body. That is the class of fault iterations 2 and 3 spent their content on: a body's type unrelated to its temperature, a moon's mass unrelated to its planet's ([decision 0051](0051-grammar-version-3-orbits-scaled-to-the-star-and-bodies-suited-to-their-zone.md)).

The project's answer to that class is a tag, and a tag is declared today on a connector: a candidate child provides tags through its own like-labelled connector declaration, and a category with no such connector provides nothing ([decision 0048](0048-composition-grammar-version-1-graph-records-and-graph-publication.md)). `surface-material`, `texture-recipe`, and `atmosphere` are leaf categories with no connector at all, so nothing they hold can be required of them. That wall is what iteration 3's first outstanding fault records ([progress](../progress.md#solar-system-iterations)).

Two other ways in were put to the owner and rejected. A connector on a leaf category that attaches nothing and exists only to carry tags is a workaround: it makes a connector mean two things, and a category registry that declares a connector no grammar may use carries a dead field. Deriving a body's appearance from its stored parameters by a named rule, in the manner of [decision 0037](0037-derivation-rules-integer-periods-and-orbit-hierarchy.md), is the cheapest and needs no registry revision, but the look would then be presentation computed from parameters rather than a primitive, which is what the invariant above refuses.

## Decision

**A definition may carry tags of its own.** They are validated as connector tags already are — canonical text, unique within the list, at most sixteen — because a tag means the same thing wherever it is declared, and they are encoded after the definition's connector declarations. Only a registry revision whose definition layout includes them carries them, which is revision 3 and later: a definition record of revision 1 or 2 is byte for byte what it was, so their recorded hashes and every pack published under them keep loading. This is the versioned-record growth of [decision 0050](0050-registry-revision-2-grammar-version-2-and-versioned-record-growth.md) applied a second time, and what makes it cost nothing is that the encoding is schema-driven and the reader dispatches on the label it opened ([decision 0020](0020-canonical-encoding-and-content-hash.md), [decision 0035](0035-category-registry-record-and-generator-revision-identifiers.md)).

**A template states the tags its definitions provide.** A definition's tags are authored, not drawn: every definition generated from a template carries that template's tags, and the template and vocabulary records carry them under the same revision gate and in the same position, after the template's connector declarations. A template is a separately versioned generation input, so what a definition provides is pinned by the vocabulary hash an iteration records ([decision 0042](0042-category-registry-revision-1-first-content-records-generator-and-publish-protocol.md)).

**A reference parameter may require tags.** The requirement is bounded variation on one parameter, so it belongs to the template's range for that parameter rather than to the registry or to any definition, which is [decision 0002](0002-primitive-set-content-gate.md)'s rule that authored content enters as templates and constraints and never as set members. The generator fills the reference only from definitions whose own tags satisfy every tag required; where no definition of the category does, it fails by name — the template, the parameter, and the tags asked for — and substitutes nothing, exactly as a reference no earlier definition supplies already fails at once ([decision 0042](0042-category-registry-revision-1-first-content-records-generator-and-publish-protocol.md)) and as a required child that cannot be placed fails ([decision 0048](0048-composition-grammar-version-1-graph-records-and-graph-publication.md)). The retry budget bounds duplicate content, as before, not this. A required tag reaches only the reference it is stated on: a definition's tags are not compared at composition, where a parent's connector requirement and a band's demand are still answered by the child's like-labelled connector declaration ([decision 0048](0048-composition-grammar-version-1-graph-records-and-graph-publication.md), [decision 0051](0051-grammar-version-3-orbits-scaled-to-the-star-and-bodies-suited-to-their-zone.md)).

A reference names a category, not a domain. A domain governs where a definition may be composed as an instance ([glossary](../glossary.md#content-identity)), which a referenced definition is not, so a `planet` of the solar-system domain may reference a `surface-material` of the surface and artifact domains exactly as that material already references a `texture-recipe`.

**Registry revision 3** is revision 2's categories with one addition: `planet` gains a `surface` reference to `surface-material`, so what a body looks like is stored content of the body. Nothing else changes, as nothing but the derived parameters changed from revision 1 to revision 2.

**Grammar version 4** lays out version 3's rules unchanged over registry revision 3. A grammar record names the registry revision whose categories and connectors it speaks of ([decision 0048](0048-composition-grammar-version-1-graph-records-and-graph-publication.md)), and the newest grammar is selected by that revision, so a registry revision with no grammar over it composes nothing. Registry revisions 1 and 2 and grammar versions 1 to 3 stay supported, so all three earlier iterations reproduce from their records ([decision 0050](0050-registry-revision-2-grammar-version-2-and-versioned-record-growth.md), [decision 0051](0051-grammar-version-3-orbits-scaled-to-the-star-and-bodies-suited-to-their-zone.md)).

**The vocabulary is authored for the newest revision**, as [decision 0050](0050-registry-revision-2-grammar-version-2-and-versioned-record-growth.md) requires: the `basic` templates tag each `surface-material` with what it suits and require those tags of the body templates' `surface` reference. Which labels those are is authored content, pinned by the vocabulary hash iteration 4 records, and the coupling is the one decision 0051 already named — a requirement no template provides fails the run rather than filling the reference with something unsuitable.

**A body's appearance becomes generated content in the database.** The Godot adapter builds a body's material from the `surface-material` the body stores and the `texture-recipe` that material references, which is what M0a exit criterion 11 asks of the viewer: resolve the material, texture, and parameter primitives through the adapter, and never treat an engine resource as the authoritative definition ([decision 0031](0031-primitive-complete-composition-and-storage.md)). Geometry and collision primitives stay exercised only by the artifact domain, whose parts carry a geometry reference; a `star` and an `atmosphere` declare no appearance under revision 3 either, so a view that stands in for them says so ([primitives](../primitives.md#part-4-why-this-is-the-universal-base)).

**This is solar-system iteration 4.** It changes the content the solar-system level generates, so under [decision 0047](0047-usable-storage-and-structure-generation-then-solar-system-iterations.md) it is a numbered iteration with its own row in [progress](../progress.md#solar-system-iterations), pinning registry revision 3 and its hash, the vocabulary hash, grammar version 4, the generator and description versions, the suit profile, and the same seed list, checked mechanically against the tier rules of [decision 0044](0044-tiered-system-composition-one-star-and-explorability-by-validation.md) and read by the owner from the sample. The owner accepted iteration 3 on 2026-09-10 with three faults named and outstanding, and this mechanism is the way in to the first of them: an atmosphere is a leaf category that has tags of its own for the first time, so what it needs of the body beneath it can be stated as data rather than left to a derivation rule. Which requirement consults those tags — a reference, as here, or a connector rule, which would extend decision 0048's matching rule — is iteration 4's question and is not settled here.

## Consequences

Easier: a body's look is generated, stored, content-addressed, and reproduced like every other property, so the viewer resolves it instead of inventing it and the primitive-completeness invariant holds for the first level that is judged by pictures; a leaf primitive can finally say what it suits, so the fault class the last two iterations fixed between bodies can be fixed between a body and what it carries; and the mechanism is data in a template, so the next tuning is a vocabulary, not code.

Harder: a third registry revision and a fourth grammar version join the two and three already retained, and every command still selects by what the content pins rather than by what the build prefers; the vocabulary must provide every tag a reference demands, a coupling between two authored files that only generation reveals; a tag now means two things in two places, provided by a connector at composition and by a definition at generation, and only the second is compared for a reference; and the appearance a body stores is drawn from a pool by tag, so a tag vocabulary too coarse gives every icy body the same face, which only the owner's reading of the sample will show.

Another frozen content format lands before the still-required P0 exit record. Registry revision 3, grammar version 4, and the definition, template, and vocabulary layouts that carry tags all change records whose hashes mint pack identifiers, which [decision 0022](0022-p0-gates-the-first-frozen-content-format.md) would have held until that record and [decision 0030](0030-m0a-criterion-1-proceeds-before-the-p0-exit-record.md) released once already, [decision 0047](0047-usable-storage-and-structure-generation-then-solar-system-iterations.md) extending the same cost to the iterations. Every set, graph, and destination minted under revision 3 may be superseded by P0's findings, exactly as those records describe; the P0 exit record itself is unaffected and still due.

Verified by the Primitive-set foundation and Determinism checks in the [development plan](../development-plan.md#verification-and-performance-targets): the frozen hashes of registry revision 3 and grammar version 4 beside the earlier ones; a revision 2 definition record still hashing to its recorded value; a reference no tagged definition satisfies failing by name with nothing published; and iteration 4's own recorded vectors, on Linux and Windows. [Progress](../progress.md#m0a-exit-criteria) owns which of those hold.
