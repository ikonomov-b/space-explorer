# 0063. Relief by `terrain-heightfield/1`, registry revision 6's relief field, and grammar version 7

Date: 2026-09-11. Status: Accepted. Source: the owner's instruction of 2026-09-11 to continue with the planned iterations, which begins rung 2 of the ladder [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 2 names; put the drafted record before him with every open clause's recommendation named, he accepted it as drafted the same day, on the instruction to continue. Carries the registry revision and grammar version that rung needs in the same record, on the precedent of [decision 0055](0055-definition-level-tags-and-tagged-references.md) and of decision 0061 itself. Supersedes the stored-grid form of rung 2 in the ladder of [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md), for the recipe form [decision 0041](0041-planet-fields-tangent-regions-minimum-radius-and-far-field.md) equally permits.

## Context

Rung 1 is closed: registry revision 5, grammar version 6, one flat tangent region per admitted body, accepted by the owner on 2026-09-11 with the material's close-up repeat named and open ([progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations)). Its ground is flat by construction and not by simplification, which is why rung 2 exists: relief is the first thing that makes one region differ from another, and [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 8 already returns the sample to the full twenty-four lever seeds here for that reason.

Rung 2's row cites [decision 0041](0041-planet-fields-tangent-regions-minimum-radius-and-far-field.md) for the grid kind, the fields, and parent-to-child sampling, and decisions [0010](0010-units-coordinates-and-region-bounds.md) and [0017](0017-region-extent-cap-and-storage-derivation.md) for the resolution, and names three things it still needs: a named relief rule, the storage policy per category, and the payload record class. Decision 0061's own deferral table repeats all three with a pointer each, and conditions the payload class on whether the planet's relief grid is stored. This record answers the three together, because the answer to the first decides the other two.

**What the documents already say, before this record argues with any of it.** Decision 0041 says global relief is a cube-sphere grid "or recipes that produce them", owned by planet instances, which regions sample as parent-to-child data flow. Decision 0061's rung-2 row names only the grid half of that sentence. [Primitives part 5](../primitives.md#where-it-must-grow) names a bulk parameter kind and a per-instance payload record class as two of the three things the structure does not yet carry, and [its arithmetic](../primitives.md#the-arithmetic-that-decides-it) already concludes that "regeneration is mandatory for terrain and the evictable cache is the load-bearing mechanism rather than the payload record — the payload holds what a player has changed and what regeneration cannot reproduce, not the ground itself." [Decision 0034](0034-storage-policy-pinned-by-manifests.md) puts the permitted policies in the category registry and the choice among them in a manifest. This record takes the recipe half of decision 0041's sentence and the conclusion primitives part 5 already reached, and shows that together they remove the need for both of the missing pieces at this rung.

**The arithmetic, from constants already fixed.** A stored relief grid at the 2 m cells rung 2 asks for costs, on a body of [decision 0041](0041-planet-fields-tangent-regions-minimum-radius-and-far-field.md)'s minimum landable radius, 3.454 × 10^12 m² of surface at 4 m² a cell, so 8.64 × 10^11 int16 samples, or **1.57 TiB for one planet**. A grid that would fit is a different object: six faces at 2^10 per face is 12 MiB, but its cells are 741 m across on that body and 9.0 km on an Earth-sized one, 370 and 4,500 times coarser than the rung asks for. The grid form cannot carry rung 2's resolution at any body size, and the form that can is the recipe.

## Decision

Each clause below stands alone, so the owner can accept or amend one at a time. A clause marked **open** is a choice this record puts to him, with the recommendation named; a clause marked **follows** applies an accepted record and is open only in the sense that anything is. Nothing here is implemented.

**1. The planet's relief is a recipe, not a stored grid (open; recommended).** A planet instance owns a relief *field* defined by a handful of stored parameters and evaluated on demand at any point, rather than a stored array of samples. The arithmetic above is the whole argument: the grid form is 1.57 TiB at the resolution asked for and 370 times too coarse at the size that fits, so the choice between decision 0041's two permitted forms is not a preference. Consequently rung 2 builds **no bulk parameter kind and no cube-sphere grid kind** — [primitives part 5](../primitives.md#where-it-must-grow)'s first gap is not on rung 2's path, because a continuous field needs no face layout, no seam, and no pole. That is [decision 0024](0024-tests-comments-index-and-scaffolding-trimmed.md)'s rule applied to a format: the kind arrives with the first category that stores one, which is still no category.

The alternative is to store a coarse grid per planet and add detail from a recipe on top, which is two mechanisms where one suffices and buys nothing a reader of rung 2 could see.

**2. The rule is `terrain-heightfield/1`, a generator revision identifier (open; recommended).** It is named in the form [decision 0035](0035-category-registry-record-and-generator-revision-identifiers.md) fixes, `<generator>/<k>`, and it is exactly the identifier that record's own docstring uses as its example. It is deliberately **not** a `derive-<name>/<k>` rule of [decision 0037](0037-derivation-rules-integer-periods-and-orbit-hierarchy.md): those have "one typed output" and stand where an aggregation rule would, for a scalar parameter of one node, and this produces a million samples for a category. Decision 0035's sentence is written for this case — "the registry lists, per category that permits `regenerate` or `hybrid`, the identifiers that may produce it" — and registry revision 6 lists it against `region`, which clause 9 turns into the storage answer.

It is shaped on [decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md) in every other respect: typed inputs, integer arithmetic throughout, no floating-point value admitted anywhere, a seed that is a reduction of a content hash, a frozen implementation, and recorded vectors. Changing any constant, the octave construction, the reduction, or the output layout is `terrain-heightfield/2`, and nothing changes under version 1.

**3. The field is addressed in the planet-fixed frame (open; recommended).** The rule evaluates the field at the planet-fixed position of each sample, so the field is a property of the planet instance and not of the region: two regions on one planet agree where they meet, and the far field of [decision 0041](0041-planet-fields-tangent-regions-minimum-radius-and-far-field.md) extends the same field past the region edge without a second rule. This is what decision 0041 means by parent-to-child sampling, and it is why rung 5 stays what decision 0061's ladder calls it, "nothing of substance; this rung is building".

The alternative is to evaluate the field in the region's own tangent frame, seeded per region. It is cheaper now — it needs no sine and no planet-fixed frame — and it is wrong at rung 5, where the far field would have to invent ground that does not continue the region's, so rung 2's accepted sample would be re-read under `terrain-heightfield/2`. The cost of the recommendation is one integer sine, which clause 5 shows is owed anyway.

**4. The planet-fixed frame and the region's mapping into it (follows, from decisions 0036 and 0041).** Nothing states the planet-fixed axes, and everything below depends on them, so they are fixed here: **+Z** is the body's north pole, **+X** pierces the prime meridian at the equator, **+Y** completes a right-handed set. With latitude φ and longitude λ as the binary turns [decision 0036](0036-frames-and-transforms-typed-by-connector-kind.md) gives the surface anchor,

- `Up = (cos φ cos λ, cos φ sin λ, sin φ)`, `North = (−sin φ cos λ, −sin φ sin λ, cos φ)`, `East = (−sin λ, cos λ, 0)`;
- with heading ψ clockwise from north, the region-local axes of decision 0041 are `X̂ = East cos ψ − North sin ψ`, `Ŷ = Up`, `Ẑ = −(North cos ψ + East sin ψ)`, so `−Ẑ` points along the heading and `X̂ × Ŷ = Ẑ`;
- a sample at region-local `(x, z)` in metres sits at planet-fixed `P = R·Up + x·X̂ + z·Ẑ`, in the 1/256 m units of [decision 0010](0010-units-coordinates-and-region-bounds.md), where `R` is the reference radius `derive-radius/1` gives the body ([decision 0050](0050-registry-revision-2-grammar-version-2-and-versioned-record-growth.md)).

`P` is not projected back onto the sphere. That is decision 0041's flat model exactly, whose error it already accepts and bounds: the region's corner sits 2.0 m off the sphere at the minimum radius, so two adjacent regions evaluate a shared edge point up to 2 m apart radially, which at clause 6's finest octave is a disagreement under a metre. Whether rung 5 pays an integer square root to make a seam exact is named in the deferral table and is not rung 2's question, because rung 2 has no two adjacent regions.

**5. Integer sine and cosine over binary turns, frozen once and shared (open; recommended).** Clause 4 needs `sin` and `cos` of three binary turns per region — six evaluations for a whole region, not one per sample. [Decision 0037](0037-derivation-rules-integer-periods-and-orbit-hierarchy.md) already requires "a table-driven sine and cosine" in fixed point for `propagate-two-body/1` at rung 6, so this is the first consumer of a mechanism an accepted record already owes, not scaffolding.

It is one shared frozen implementation in Core, and rung 6 uses the same one. That is not a breach of [decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md)'s "no other code may call them": that prohibition is on *hash and mixer* constructions, which bear identity and whose second instance is a determinism hazard, and a sine is arithmetic of the kind `DerivationRules`' integer square and cube roots already are. What the two cases share is the freeze — the first stored or vectored result under this sine fixes it, and changing it afterwards is a version change of every rule that calls it.

Its construction, chosen over a baked table because a table of 1,025 literals is unreadable and over computing one from `Math.Sin` because that admits a floating-point value into an authoritative path:

- `Sin(t)` for an int32 binary turn `t`, at scale 2^30. Take `u = (uint)t`, `q = u >> 30`, `w = u & 0x3FFFFFFF`. For `q` of 0, 1, 2, 3 the answer is `Q(w)`, `Q(2^30 − w)`, `−Q(w)`, `−Q(2^30 − w)`. `Cos(t) = Sin(t + 2^30)`.
- `Q(w)` for `w` in `[0, 2^30]` is `SinPoly(w)` where `w ≤ 2^29` and `CosPoly(2^30 − w)` above it, so the polynomial argument never exceeds an eighth turn.
- Both polynomials take `X = (a × TAU) >> 32`, the angle in radians at scale 2^32, with `TAU = round(2π × 2^32) = 26,986,075,409`, and `X2 = (X × X) >> 2`, the square at scale 2^62. `SinPoly` is Horner over the odd terms with `acc` starting at `C11` and stepping `acc ← C(2m+1) − ((X2 × acc) >> 62)` for `m` of 4, 3, 2, 1, 0, returning `(X × acc) >> 64`; `CosPoly` is the same over the even terms from `D12` down to `D0`, returning `acc >> 32`. Every coefficient is derived rather than tabulated: `Ck = round(2^62 / k!)` and `Dk = round(2^62 / k!)`. Intermediates are 128-bit, as `DerivationRules` already is.
- Truncation after the 2^62-scaled `x^11` term leaves an error under 10^−10 at an eighth turn, which is under a unit at scale 2^30.

**6. `terrain-heightfield/1`, in full (open as to the constants; recommended).**

*Inputs.* One `relief-field/1` record (clause 7); the region's anchor latitude, longitude, and heading as int32 binary turns; and the region's stored `extent` in metres. The cell size is [decision 0010](0010-units-coordinates-and-region-bounds.md)'s 2 m and is not an input.

*Output.* `n × n` int16 heights at 1/16 m, `n = extent / 2 + 1`, in row order with `z` outer and `x` inner: sample `(i, j)` is the ground at region-local `x = (i − (n−1)/2) × 2` and `z = (j − (n−1)/2) × 2` metres, and its value is the region-local **+Y** coordinate of the ground there, positive outward. A maximal region is 1,025 × 1,025 samples, 2,101,250 bytes. That is 0.195% over the 2 MiB decision 0017 derives from 1,024 × 1,024 cells, and the two agree rather than differ: a biome index belongs to a cell and a height to the lattice corner shared by four of them, so a field that closes both edges carries one extra row and column. The convention is chosen for the rung it is not yet at — two adjacent regions share that row, so nothing has to be invented at a seam.

*The seed.* The leading eight bytes of the `relief-field/1` record's content hash, read big-endian with the low bit set. No other seed is admitted, which is [decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md)'s construction unchanged.

*The lattice value.* For integer lattice cell `(i, j, k)` at octave `o`, with unchecked 64-bit wrapping,

```
h = seed ^ (i × 0xD6E8FEB86659FD93) ^ (j × 0xA24BAED4963EE407)
         ^ (k × 0x9FB21C651E98DF25) ^ (o × 0x2545F4914F6CDD1D)
h ^= h >> 30;  h ×= 0xBF58476D1CE4E5B9
h ^= h >> 27;  h ×= 0x94D049BB133111EB
h ^= h >> 31
value = (long)(h >> 48) − 32768                      // −32,768 .. 32,767
```

The four multipliers are this rule's own and none of them is one of decision 0056's three, so the two rules cannot be entangled by a shared constant. The finalizer is SplitMix64's, which is published and independently checkable — `finalize(0x9E3779B97F4A7C15)` is `0xE220A8397B1DCDAF`, SplitMix64's first output for seed 0 — so this rule's one hash construction rests on a vector computed outside this repository, which is the thing decision 0056 admits `derive-surface-raster/1` cannot claim.

*The octaves.* The coarsest wavelength is the field's stored `relief-wavelength` `W` in metres and each octave halves it. The count is derived and not chosen: `octaves = 1 + log2(W / 4)`, so the finest wavelength is always 4 m, twice decision 0010's cell, which is the finest a 2 m lattice can carry. `W` between 4 m and 8,192 m therefore gives 1 to 12 octaves.

*One octave's contribution.* At octave `o` with wavelength `L`, the lattice coordinate of a planet-fixed axis value `p` in 1/256 m is `q = floorDiv(p << 8, L)` at scale 2^16 — floor division, so the lattice is continuous across zero — with cell index `q >> 16` and fraction `f = q & 0xFFFF`. The interpolation weight is the integer smoothstep `(f × f × (3 × 65536 − 2 × f)) >> 32`, which is exactly 0, 32768, and 65536 at 0, a half, and 1. Eight corner values are combined trilinearly by `a + (((b − a) × w) >> 16)`, four along x, two along y, one along z.

*The sum.* Octave amplitudes are normalised so they total exactly the stored amplitude, whatever the roughness: with `r` the stored `relief-roughness` as a fraction of 65,535, take `w_o = r^o` at scale 2^16, `W_sum = Σ w_o`, and `a_o = A₁₆ × w_o / W_sum` in 1/16 m, where `A₁₆` is the stored `relief-amplitude` in 1/16 m. The height is `Σ (v_o × a_o) / 32768`, every division rounding half away from zero as `DerivationRules` already does. The bound follows: `|height| ≤ A₁₆`, so an amplitude capped at 1,024 m uses half of what int16 at 1/16 m holds and cannot saturate. The other half is deliberate headroom for the biome-modulated relief of rung 3 and the far field of rung 5.

*What the rule does not do.* It fixes its output and not its evaluation: an implementation may cache corner values between neighbouring samples, evaluate octaves in any order, or run the rows in parallel, so long as the bytes are identical.

**7. `relief-field/1`: a frozen canonical record, per planet instance (open; recommended).** Under [decision 0020](0020-canonical-encoding-and-content-hash.md), with domain label `relief-field/1` and fields in this order: the body's reference radius in 1/256 m; `relief-amplitude` in metres; `relief-roughness` as a fraction of 65,535; `relief-wavelength` in metres; the graph specification's composition seed; and the planet's instance path. Its content hash is the field's identity and seeds the rule.

The last two fields are what make the field belong to a planet **instance** rather than to a planet definition, which is what decision 0041 says and what the sample needs: a definition's parameters are drawn once per set, so the `basic` vocabulary's 36 bodies would otherwise give 36 landscapes across every destination, and two instances of one definition in one system would carry the same ground. Both inputs are already inside the graph record and covered by its hash, so nothing new is pinned and no stream is derived: this follows [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 14's rule that the parent's path is inherited and the parent's stream is not, by inheriting the path and taking no draw at all.

**8. Registry revision 6 and grammar version 7 (open as to their content; follows as to their form).** Revision 6 is revision 5 with three parameters added to the `planet` category and one change to `region`:

| Parameter | Kind | Unit | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `relief-amplitude` | integer | m | 0 to 1,024 | 128 |
| `relief-roughness` | integer | ratio of 65,535 | 0 to 65,535 | 32,768 |
| `relief-wavelength` | integer | m | 4 to 8,192 | 2,048 |

Each carries its unit, its default and the decoder's bounds inside the record, which is the standard [finding 50](../review.md#50-a-stored-quantity-carries-no-unit-no-default-and-no-validation-limit) set and [decision 0060](0060-registry-revision-4-units-defaults-and-validation-limits.md) built. The amplitude's cap is derived in clause 6 and not chosen; the wavelength's floor is the finest octave and its cap is twelve octaves; the roughness needs no cap because clause 6 normalises.

Grammar version 7 lays version 6's rules over revision 6 and adds no production: a planet still carries one region wherever `admit-landable-body/1` admits one, and the anchor's height component stays pinned at zero, because clause 6's heights are measured from the tangent plane at the reference radius and need no second datum. Revisions 1 to 5 and grammar versions 1 to 6 stay supported, so rung 1 and every solar-system iteration reproduce from their records ([decision 0050](0050-registry-revision-2-grammar-version-2-and-versioned-record-growth.md)). The vocabulary is authored for the newest revision, so its hash moves and re-mints the set pack and every destination, which is free because iteration content is disposable ([decision 0047](0047-usable-storage-and-structure-generation-then-solar-system-iterations.md)).

**9. The storage policy: `region` permits regeneration and nothing else (open; recommended).** Registry revision 6 declares the `region` category's permitted policies as `Regenerate` alone and lists `terrain-heightfield/1` as the identifier that produces it. [Decision 0034](0034-storage-policy-pinned-by-manifests.md) puts the permitted set in the category registry and the choice among them in a manifest; where a category permits one policy there is no choice to pin, so **no manifest pin is written at this rung** and the format's existing `PolicyPin` field stays empty, correctly, as it is for every category that owns no payload today. A later measurement that wants `hybrid` for terrain adds it to the permitted set in a registry revision, which is how every other change here happens.

This is the one place [primitives part 5](../primitives.md#what-is-not-yet-knowable)'s "every category of every registry revision permits `Materialize` and nothing else" stops being true, and it stops being true for the one category whose arithmetic already decided it: 804 GiB to materialise the smallest landable body against the 300 MiB decision 0017 budgets a 300-region campaign. The code already refuses the inconsistent form — `CategoryDefinition` throws unless a category lists generator revisions exactly when it permits regeneration — so the policy and the rule that honours it cannot drift apart.

**10. The payload record class stays deferred (open; recommended).** Decision 0061's deferral row conditions it on the planet's relief grid being stored, and under clauses 1 and 9 nothing is stored: the field is a recipe and the heightfield is regenerated. [Primitives part 5](../primitives.md#the-arithmetic-that-decides-it)'s own sentence names what the class is for — "the payload holds what a player has changed and what regeneration cannot reproduce, not the ground itself" — so the first thing that needs it is the mutable-state overlay of [decision 0038](0038-derived-instances.md), not relief. Building a record class, a writer, and an index table to hold 2 MiB that a rule reproduces in a second would be the scaffolding decisions [0002](0002-primitive-set-content-gate.md) and [0024](0024-tests-comments-index-and-scaffolding-trimmed.md) refuse.

Determinism does not argue the other way, which is the objection worth answering: a content hash is not the only way to prove two machines agree. Clause 13's recorded digest of a full field is compared on `ubuntu-latest` and `windows-latest` exactly as `derive-surface-raster/1`'s image digest is, and that image is stored nowhere either ([decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md)).

**11. The view draws the relief, and says what it supplied (open as to the frames' content; follows as to the principle).** `src/SpaceExplorer.Game/SurfaceView.cs` replaces its `PlaneMesh` with a mesh built from the heightfield: 1,025² vertices at the sample positions of clause 6, displaced by the stored height with **no vertical exaggeration**, indexed into 1,024² quads, its texture coordinates unchanged so the material still tiles at the length the recipe's scale claims, and its normals taken from central differences over the field. The material comes from `GroundFor` unchanged. Collision is not built: rung 2 has no player physics, and the region edge is the traversal primitive's rule at rung 5 ([decision 0041](0041-planet-fields-tangent-regions-minimum-radius-and-far-field.md)).

The walk frame's eye **rides the ground**: its height is the field sampled bilinearly under the camera plus [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 7's 2 m, still held inside the region's stored extent ([decision 0062](0062-the-surface-harness-walks-in-an-interactive-session.md) clause 4, unchanged). Walking is how relief is judged, and a camera at a fixed altitude over a landscape reports the altitude and not the landscape.

The map frame keeps its fixed orthographic pose and gains a **height key** beside the scale bar, naming the lowest and the highest sample in metres. The scale bar exists because a reader must be able to measure the ground rather than guess it, and at rung 2 that argument applies to the vertical axis for the first time.

The legend moves relief onto the stored line and names what the harness still supplies, which is [decision 0057](0057-the-view-draws-what-the-content-stores.md)'s principle as decision 0061 clause 7 and decision 0062 clause 5 already apply it. Stored and derived: the extent, the material, the texture scale, the anchor, the three relief parameters as the record gives them, the octave count, the relief field's hash, and the field's range across this region. Supplied: the light direction, the sky colour, the eye height, the pace, and now the shading normals — with the vertical exaggeration stated as none, because a reader who is asked to judge amplitude must be told the picture does not stretch it.

One risk is named rather than hidden: one mesh of 1,025² vertices is some 2.1 million triangles, and nothing chunks it until rung 7 ([open items](../progress.md#open-items-outside-the-plans-process)). If the workstation cannot hold that at a readable rate, the harness draws every second sample and the legend says so as a supplied simplification — which is a reading the sample must not be given silently.

**12. The sample is the full twenty-four lever seeds (follows).** [Decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 8 already says the sample returns to twenty-four at this rung, for this reason. Beside the owner's verdict the row prints the counts of clause 11 of that record — regions per destination and distinct surface materials — and adds a third of the same cheap kind, **distinct relief fields across the sample**, which is [finding 55](../review.md#55-uniqueness-is-unguarded-above-the-artifact)'s recommended step applied to the thing this rung adds: make repetition visible rather than corrected. The pins are decision 0061 clause 10's, with `terrain-heightfield`'s version added beside `derive-surface-raster`'s, because a verdict read from pictures is a verdict on both rules' output.

**13. What proves it (follows, on the precedents of decisions 0020, 0049 and 0056).** Four vectors, no two of which rest on the same thing.

- **A hand case.** A 4 × 4 field from a named `relief-field/1` record at one octave, all sixteen values written out in the suite, each following from the constants of clause 6 and computed by an implementation outside this repository. That is [decision 0020](0020-canonical-encoding-and-content-hash.md)'s 47-byte vector applied to a rule.
- **A published anchor.** `finalize(0x9E3779B97F4A7C15) = 0xE220A8397B1DCDAF`, and the sine of an eighth, a sixth, a quarter and a half turn against their published values to within a unit at scale 2^30. Neither rests on this repository's code, which is the improvement over `derive-surface-raster/1`'s recorded digest that [decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md) names as its own weakness.
- **A portability digest.** The SHA-256 of a full 1,025² field for one named record and one named anchor, compared on `ubuntu-latest` and `windows-latest`, which is what makes the landscape portable rather than only repeatable here.
- **Two properties.** Every sample within `±relief-amplitude`, so clause 6's bound is load-bearing and not a claim; and two anchors 1,024 m apart on one planet agreeing at the points they share to within the metre clause 4 derives from decision 0041's accepted flat-model error, which is the whole of clause 3's argument stated as a test.

Mutation-checked, as every rule here is: dropping the octave normalisation, taking truncating instead of floor division for a negative lattice coordinate, seeding the field from the definition rather than from the instance, and reading the field in the region's frame rather than the planet's should each turn the suite red.

**14. The first generation cost worth timing (open; recommended).** Every timing taken so far is dominated by a tenth of a second of process startup, and the 60-second starter destination and useful-content-per-second of [decision 0004](0004-preparation-is-real-generation-time.md) are untracked ([progress](../progress.md#verification-and-performance-targets)). One maximal region is 1,050,625 samples at up to twelve octaves and eight corners each, so of the order of 10^8 lattice evaluations — the first piece of this project's generation that is real work. Rung 2 therefore records the wall-clock cost of one maximal region on the reference workstation in the Resource-use row. It is one number on one machine and settles no threshold; it is the first number decision 0004's measure can ever rest on, and taking it now costs a stopwatch.

**15. What rung 2 does not make fallible (follows).** [Decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) defers the per-phase retry and refund rule to "when the surface phase can fail, which is rung 2 or 3". It is not rung 2: `terrain-heightfield/1` is total — every admitted body and every anchor yields a field — so no region can fail and nothing retries. The deferral stands, to rung 3 or 4. The landing-candidate flag and description version 3 are likewise untouched, for the reason decision 0061 clause 12 gives: a region still cannot pass or fail the suit profile.

## Consequences

Easier: the three things rung 2 was said to need turn out to be one thing and two absences — a rule, no grid kind, and no payload class — because the arithmetic that already forbids materialising terrain also forbids storing the grid it would be materialised from, and both follow from constants decisions 0010, 0017 and 0041 fixed long ago. Relief is a property of a planet instance rather than of a view or a region, so the far field extends it, a chunk at rung 7 samples it, and two regions on one planet are two places on one world. The heightfield rule's one hash construction has a published anchor, which `derive-surface-raster/1` never had. And the first honest generation cost in this project becomes measurable at the moment the first real generation exists.

Harder: a sixth registry revision and a seventh grammar version join the five and six retained, and every command still selects by what the content pins; the published revision 5 content cannot carry relief, so cycle two re-mints its set and its destinations again; the harness gains a million-vertex mesh with no level of detail until rung 7; and `terrain-heightfield/1` and the integer sine are retained implementations for as long as any supported revision lists them ([decision 0035](0035-category-registry-record-and-generator-revision-identifiers.md)), which is a permanent obligation taken on knowingly.

**A known cost, chosen again.** This is the second of the four rungs [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md)'s Consequences say will each fire [finding 54](../review.md#54-the-registry-the-grammars-and-the-numeric-tables-are-code-where-the-vocabulary-is-content)'s trigger: the registry revision, the grammar version and the rule are C# where the vocabulary is data, so tuning relief between cycles is a rebuild where tuning a colour is a file edit. Finding 54 stays open and this record clears nothing of it. The three relief parameters are content, though, and their ranges live in the authored vocabulary, so what the owner is most likely to want to tune after reading the sample — how high, how rough, how large the features — is the half that is already data.

**What must be verified**, on the evidence [progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations) records: the four vectors of clause 13 and the mutations beside them; registry revision 6's and grammar version 7's frozen hashes beside the earlier ones, with every earlier revision's and version's hash unmoved; a `region` category that permits regeneration and lists its producing identifier, and the refusal of one that does not; two processes composing the same graph byte for byte; the description's version 3 vector unmoved, which is clause 15's claim; the cost figure of clause 14; and the exported-build smoke check still printing `SMOKE OK`, which a review path must not disturb. The Primitive-set foundation, Determinism and Resource-use checks of the [development plan](../development-plan.md#verification-and-performance-targets) own those; Surface-sky consistency stays at Not started until rung 6.
