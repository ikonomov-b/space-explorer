# 0071. What every surface iteration must do from now on: the explorable planet is its subject, the far field comes before features, and the inspection is two windows

Date: 2026-09-12. Status: Accepted. Source: the owner's instruction of 2026-09-12, given before the next surface iteration, in six statements: that each iteration must start by generating a solar system with one explorable planet and generating that planet's surface; that everything generated must be stored in the database and rendered from it; that the far parts of the generated planet surface should be generalized instead of cut; that the inspection should show the solar system in one window and that same destination's explorable planet surface in a second; that each generation must be unique; and that the explorable planet's type should be picked at random too. Put to him clause by clause with every open choice's recommendation named, he settled the ladder order first — the rungs swap, and the far field is next — and then the rest, accepting each recommendation as drafted on 2026-09-12. Supersedes the rung order of [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 2 for rungs 4 and 5, and the sweep selection of [decision 0070](0070-a-biome-is-a-derived-set-registry-revision-9-and-grammar-version-10.md) clause 12; amends the region clause of [decision 0044](0044-tiered-system-composition-one-star-and-explorability-by-validation.md) by citation; changes nothing of decisions [0066](0066-generation-writes-to-the-database-and-the-view-only-reads.md), [0067](0067-a-destinations-ground-is-generated-when-it-is-composed.md), or the rest of 0070. Raises [findings 56](../review.md#56-a-starter-destination-grounds-every-admitted-body-where-decision-0044-gives-its-explorable-planet-one-region), which clause 3 clears, and [57](../review.md#57-the-explorable-planets-type-is-drawn-but-only-rock-and-ocean-can-win-it), which stays open.

## Context

Three of the six statements are already accepted policy and are recorded here as citations rather than as new rules; three are changes to accepted records and are the reason this record exists. Which is which was checked against the build and against the data root, not inferred from the documents alone.

**Measured, on the reference workstation against `52405eb`,** by composing or loading the starter destination of each of lever seeds 1 to 24 over set pack `e5565984` (registry revision 7, grammar version 8, the pins of [surface cycle three](../progress.md#surface-iterations)): every one of the 24 destinations passes the starter tier with **exactly one explorable planet**, as [decision 0044](0044-tiered-system-composition-one-star-and-explorability-by-validation.md) requires. Those 24 planets are **18 rocky and 6 ocean, and none icy**. Beside them the same destinations carry **37 landable moons**, every one of them rocky — 61 landing candidates in all, a mean of 2.5 to a destination, where the tier rule counts only the planets. The ground under all of them is larger still: the grammar places a region on every body `admit-landable-body/1` admits, which is a mean of 4.6 regions to a destination ([progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations)), so most stored ground is on bodies the suit refuses and cycle one's reading already found four of its six frames standing on one.

## Decision

Each clause stands alone, so the owner could accept or amend one at a time; he accepted all of them on 2026-09-12. A clause marked **the owner's choice** is one this record put to him with a recommendation, which he took; a clause marked **follows** applies an accepted record and is written down so no later cycle drops it.

**1. A cycle begins by composing a starter destination and grounding it (follows; statement 1's first half).** A surface iteration starts by composing, for each lever seed of its sample, the starter-tier destination those levers name — which by [decision 0044](0044-tiered-system-composition-one-star-and-explorability-by-validation.md) and tier profile version 1 ([decision 0058](0058-tier-rules-are-a-pinned-record.md)) has exactly one explorable planet, the bounded retry of [decision 0052](0052-the-two-levers-compose-a-destination-and-a-review-view-shows-it.md) drawing again until it does — and publishing it, which generates and writes every region's ground before the destination record is indexed ([decision 0067](0067-a-destinations-ground-is-generated-when-it-is-composed.md) clause 1). That is what the sweep of [tools](../tools.md#godot) already does and what the measurement above re-ran; nothing changes. It is recorded because the alternative is available and wrong for a surface cycle: `iterate` and `destination --no-publish` compose without storing, which is the right shape for a solar-system iteration reading text and the wrong one for a cycle whose subject is ground.

**2. The subject of a surface cycle is the destination's explorable planet (the owner's choice of 2026-09-12; supersedes [decision 0070](0070-a-biome-is-a-derived-set-registry-revision-9-and-grammar-version-10.md) clause 12).** The frames of a cycle are taken on the region of the body the tier rule made explorable — the one planet whose description says `landing candidate` — and not on the destination's first region, nor on a region chosen by rotating the body kind through the seed index. Where a destination's explorable planet carries no region the run says so by name and draws the first region it has, as the kind selection already falls back today.

This is statement 1's second half read literally, and statement 4's, and it is what statement 6 asks for by another route: the type of ground a reader sees becomes the type the generator drew for the planet a player would land on, which is the distribution that matters, rather than a type the harness selected to make the sample look varied. Clause 12 of [decision 0070](0070-a-biome-is-a-derived-set-registry-revision-9-and-grammar-version-10.md) was the right answer to cycle three's fault — forty-eight frames of rocky ground for the cycle whose change was that ground varies by body — but it fixes the reading by choosing what to read, and the measurement above says what that hides: **no icy body is ever a landing candidate**, so the rotation has been showing the reader a third of its frames on ground no player of the core release can stand on. That is [finding 57](../review.md#57-the-explorable-planets-type-is-drawn-but-only-rock-and-ocean-can-win-it), and it is content's to fix, not the harness's.

The rotation is superseded outright and not kept beside it. The alternative — 24 frames of the subject and a handful by kind, as cycle three's reading already had to render six by name — was put to the owner and refused, so **the next cycle's sample shows no icy ground**: it is 18 rocky and 6 ocean on the measurement above, where the rotation gave 8 frames of each kind. That is accepted as a consequence and not held as a gap to be filled first. It is the true distribution of ground a player of the core release can stand on, and its narrowness is [finding 57](../review.md#57-the-explorable-planets-type-is-drawn-but-only-rock-and-ocean-can-win-it), which stays open and blocks nothing: a fault of the content, made visible rather than papered over by a sweep that chooses what to show.

**3. Ground stays on every admitted body, and decision 0044's region clause is amended by citation (the owner's choice of 2026-09-12; amends [decision 0044](0044-tiered-system-composition-one-star-and-explorability-by-validation.md), clearing [finding 56](../review.md#56-a-starter-destination-grounds-every-admitted-body-where-decision-0044-gives-its-explorable-planet-one-region)).** [Decision 0044](0044-tiered-system-composition-one-star-and-explorability-by-validation.md) says "the starter tier's explorable planet has one landing region", which reads as one region to a destination; the grammar has placed one region on every body `admit-landable-body/1` admits since version 6, which is 4.6 to a destination, most of them on bodies the suit refuses. The words are amended and the grammar is left alone. **The amendment, in the form a later reader should cite:** a region is ground, and a landing region is ground a suit survives; every body large enough by [region limits version 1](0059-region-limits-are-a-pinned-record.md) and declaring `solid-surface` carries exactly one region, and the starter tier's one explorable planet carries the one landing region decision 0044 counts. The per-tier region counts that record states for higher tiers are counts of landing regions and are untouched.

That is what the build has always done, what [decision 0067](0067-a-destinations-ground-is-generated-when-it-is-composed.md) clause 1 grounds, and what the owner's own reason for storing a destination asks for, a place revisited rather than a picture taken once ([decision 0066](0066-generation-writes-to-the-database-and-the-view-only-reads.md)). The alternative, narrowing the grammar so only the explorable planet carries a region, was put to him and refused: it would cut a destination's ground from 4.5 MiB to about 1 MiB against [decision 0068](0068-campaign-storage-budget-re-derived-from-the-payload-ceiling.md)'s 640 MiB budget, which is not a saving anything needs, it costs a grammar version and a re-mint of every destination and its ground ([decision 0067](0067-a-destinations-ground-is-generated-when-it-is-composed.md) clause 5), and it would leave the far field of clause 5 readable on one kind of body only.

**4. Everything generated is stored and drawn from the store, and the one line that is not is named (follows; statement 2).** This is already settled and nothing in it is reopened: generation writes to the database and the view only reads ([decision 0066](0066-generation-writes-to-the-database-and-the-view-only-reads.md) clause 1), the ground is written when a destination is composed so a stored destination is complete or absent ([decision 0067](0067-a-destinations-ground-is-generated-when-it-is-composed.md) clause 1), the biome patches are stored in `region-payload/2` ([decision 0070](0070-a-biome-is-a-derived-set-registry-revision-9-and-grammar-version-10.md) clause 7), and the set, the graph and the destination have been content-addressed records since decisions [0042](0042-category-registry-revision-1-first-content-records-generator-and-publish-protocol.md), [0048](0048-composition-grammar-version-1-graph-records-and-graph-publication.md) and [0053](0053-a-destination-is-a-stored-record-addressed-by-its-levers.md).

What is *not* stored is a projection: an image from a stored recipe (`derive-surface-raster`, [decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md)), a per-cell biome index from stored patches (`derive-biome-index/1`, [decision 0070](0070-a-biome-is-a-derived-set-registry-revision-9-and-grammar-version-10.md) clauses 8a and 9), a relief field from six numbers a planet already stores ([decision 0066](0066-generation-writes-to-the-database-and-the-view-only-reads.md) clause 5), and a system description from a graph. The standing rule this clause writes down for every later rung, so rung 4 does not reargue it: **anything that carries identity or consumes a random stream is stored; anything that is a pure function of stored bytes may be derived on load, and the legend says so.** If the owner means statement 2 more strictly than that — that the derived plane and the rastered image must be written too — it reverses [decision 0070](0070-a-biome-is-a-derived-set-registry-revision-9-and-grammar-version-10.md) clause 1 and [decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md) at a cost those records measured, and it is a separate record; this clause is the reading that leaves both standing.

**5. The far field comes before features: rungs 4 and 5 of the ladder swap (the owner's choice of 2026-09-12, on the recommendation below; supersedes [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 2's order).** Asked which of the two rungs statement 3 buys — reordering the ladder, extending rung 4, or a standing constraint on every rung — he took the reorder: the next cycle is rung 5, the far field and the region edge, and features, scatter and one site, follow it. Statement 3 is exactly what [decision 0041](0041-planet-fields-tangent-regions-minimum-radius-and-far-field.md) already decided and no rung has built: the adapter renders the ground between the region edge and the geometric horizon from the planet's own relief field sampled in the region's tangent frame, as an evictable cache, with the authoritative heights winning inside the region and the far field having no collision, no placements and no state. The harness today ends the world at the region's own extent and says so in code; a reader at the window is standing on a 2,048 m plate with nothing beyond it.

Four reasons stood behind the recommendation he took, recorded because a reordered ladder should not rest on preference alone. [Decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 2's own table says rung 5 "needs nothing of substance; this rung is building", where rung 4 still needs the `site component` category, the deriving categories and their budgets, and the per-phase retry and refund rule that [decision 0070](0070-a-biome-is-a-derived-set-registry-revision-9-and-grammar-version-10.md) clause 13 deferred to it — the first rung on the ladder that can fail. The far field rests on a property already proved: the relief field belongs to the planet and not to the region, which [decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md)'s rotation test pins precisely so a far field continues a region instead of inventing a second landscape beside it. The int16 headroom [decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md) clause 6 reserved has two claimants and [decision 0070](0070-a-biome-is-a-derived-set-registry-revision-9-and-grammar-version-10.md) clause 15 left it unspent for this one. And a scatter judged inside a cut horizon is judged against a false one: how much of a region a reader sees at once changes with what lies beyond it.

One thing the rung must settle that decision 0041 predates, and the owner settled it the same day, so "nothing of substance" is no longer quite true: **what the far ground is made of.** Since [decision 0070](0070-a-biome-is-a-derived-set-registry-revision-9-and-grammar-version-10.md) a region's material comes from patches drawn on the region's own stream, and that record's deferral table already names the seam — two regions of one body carry independent patches. **The far field wears the planet's own stored `surface` material**, which [decision 0070](0070-a-biome-is-a-derived-set-registry-revision-9-and-grammar-version-10.md) clause 3 keeps for exactly this kind of use and which the system view already draws a body in, with the legend naming it as supplied and naming the boundary where the region's own biomes give way to it. Deriving patches in the planet-fixed frame so the far ground carries biomes too was the other answer and is not taken: it belongs to rung 7 with chunking, where two adjacent regions must agree, and it is where that deferral stays.

**6. The inspection is two windows on one destination, and two flags are authorized rather than a harness (the owner's choice of 2026-09-12 as to the flags; follows as to the form; statement 4).** Two live windows are already achievable with what is built, and this is a [tools](../tools.md#godot) entry rather than a decision: `--system` and `--surface` are two invocations of one Godot project, both take `--tier`, `--seed`, `--set` and `--data-root`, and both load the stored destination the levers name rather than composing a second one ([decision 0053](0053-a-destination-is-a-stored-record-addressed-by-its-levers.md)), so the same levers in two processes are two windows on one destination — the system in the first and its ground in the second.

What is missing is only that the second window cannot be told to draw *the explorable planet's* region: `--region <n>` takes a graph index and `--kind` takes a body type, and neither names the body the tier rule chose, though the surface legend already prints `landing candidate` or `no: temperature` for the body it drew. Two flags are therefore authorized for building, not merely specified. **`--landable`** on the surface view selects the first region whose body is a landing candidate, which is clause 2's selection made explicit and is read from `BodyDescription.LandingCandidate`, already in the record the view loads; it is the sweep's selection from this cycle on, and where a destination's explorable planet carries no region the run says so and falls back as `--kind` already does. **`--destination <pack>`** on the surface view, which the system view already takes, lets both windows be pinned to one pack identifier when a reading is about a stored destination rather than about a pair of levers. Neither adds a rendering feature, and the two review-harness frames of [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 7 are unchanged: what the second window shows is the walk frame and the map frame it already shows.

**7. Uniqueness is already a finding, and this record adds one measurement rather than a rule (follows; statement 5).** Two generations of one pipeline can never be the same object: a set, a graph, a payload and a destination are content-addressed ([decision 0020](0020-canonical-encoding-and-content-hash.md), [decision 0053](0053-a-destination-is-a-stored-record-addressed-by-its-levers.md)), a composition seed derives from the tier, the lever seed and the attempt, and every region's relief field is seeded by the composition seed and the instance path, which is why cycle two could record every field as distinct by construction. What is unguarded is *sameness a reader would see*, and that is [finding 55](../review.md#55-uniqueness-is-unguarded-above-the-artifact), open since 2026-09-10, whose recommended first step — make repetition visible rather than corrected — is what every surface row already applies: regions per destination, distinct surface materials, distinct relief fields, distinct biome elements, biomes present per region.

Clause 2 makes one more count worth printing and makes it cheap, because it narrows the sample to 24 planets: **how many distinct planet definitions the sample's explorable planets come from.** Twenty-four grounds drawn from three definitions is the thinness iteration 4's reading found one level up, and nothing would say so. No rule is proposed here; a rule that re-draws a system for resembling another is finding 55's own open question and needs the scope, the recipe and the collision action the owner has not settled.

**8. The explorable planet's type is drawn already; what is not random is which types can win (follows as to the mechanism; the fix is content and is [finding 57](../review.md#57-the-explorable-planets-type-is-drawn-but-only-rock-and-ocean-can-win-it)).** Nothing selects the explorable planet: the grammar draws every body from the set under the zone tags of [decision 0051](0051-grammar-version-3-orbits-scaled-to-the-star-and-bodies-suited-to-their-zone.md), the description validates each against the suit profile, and whichever planet passes *is* the explorable one — its type included. So statement 6's mechanism exists and clause 2 is what puts its outcome in front of the reader. The measurement says the outcome is narrow: 18 rocky and 6 ocean over 24 seeds and never icy, because an icy body takes a cold band and is refused by temperature and by gravity together — `no: gravity temperature` at 68 K and 0.86 m/s² on seed 8. Widening it is a vocabulary and tier question — an icy body massive enough and close enough in to survive the suit, or a suit profile that admits one — and not a harness or grammar question; it is named as a finding rather than answered here, because what a world is made of is content the owner tunes ([finding 54](../review.md#54-the-registry-the-grammars-and-the-numeric-tables-are-code-where-the-vocabulary-is-content) names which half is a file edit).

## Consequences

Easier: a cycle's frames are of ground a player could stand on, so a reading judges the game's surface rather than the pipeline's; the harness stops choosing what a sample looks like, which is what made cycle three's fault invisible and would have hidden finding 57 for another rung; the next rung is the one that needs no new category, no new budget and no new failure mode, and it removes the one thing a reader at the window cannot help seeing, a world that ends 1,024 m away; and the two-window inspection costs a paragraph in [tools](../tools.md#godot) and two flags rather than a harness.

Harder: the sample loses its icy frames until finding 57 is answered in the content, which is the visibility this record chooses over the coverage the rotation gave; rung 4's own deferrals sit one cycle longer; the far field needs a material story decision 0041 predates, which clause 5 answers with the planet's stored material and a legend line at the boundary; and a second window means a reader runs two processes against one data root, which is safe for reading and is not a form anything has yet been tested in.

**What must be verified**, on the evidence [progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations) records: every seed of the sample resolving to the body its description marks `landing candidate`, and saying so where it falls back; the far field continuing the region's own heights across the edge, which is [decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md)'s rotation property read at the seam rather than a new vector; the two windows drawing one destination pack from one data root; the counts of clause 7 beside those a row already prints; and the exported-build smoke check still printing `SMOKE OK`. The Destination validity and Resource-use checks of the [development plan](../development-plan.md#verification-and-performance-targets) own those; Surface-sky consistency stays at Not started until the sky rung.
