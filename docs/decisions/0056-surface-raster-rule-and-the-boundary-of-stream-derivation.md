# 0056. A texture recipe becomes pixels in the core, by the versioned rule `derive-surface-raster/1`

Date: 2026-09-10. Status: Accepted. Source: owner decision of 2026-09-10 on the recommendation of the [inspection](../inspection.md#2-the-core-rasterizer-and-surface-appearance1-are-unrecorded-design) of the same day, whose finding 2 this clears. It records where the appearance content of [decision 0055](0055-definition-level-tags-and-tagged-references.md) is turned into an image, and draws the boundary of [decision 0021](0021-sha256-stream-derivation.md) rather than an exception to it: nothing decision 0021 fixed changes.

## Context

Iteration 4 put a body's appearance in the database, and the code that draws it added four things no record states ([inspection finding 2](../inspection.md#2-the-core-rasterizer-and-surface-appearance1-are-unrecorded-design)): a version-numbered format `surface-appearance/1` that no iteration row pins, a second mixing construction in Core after [decision 0021](0021-sha256-stream-derivation.md) deleted the first, an unnamed and unversioned algorithm that turns a `texture-recipe` into pixels, and a layering position argued only in a commit message. [Decision 0055](0055-definition-level-tags-and-tagged-references.md) says only that "the Godot adapter builds a body's material from the `surface-material` the body stores and the `texture-recipe` that material references"; where the recipe becomes pixels, under what version, and seeded by what is unwritten. A verdict on iteration 4 is read from pictures this code produced, so what produced them has to be pinned like everything else the row pins ([decision 0047](0047-usable-storage-and-structure-generation-then-solar-system-iterations.md)).

Whether a second construction is admissible at all has to be settled first. [Review finding 20](../review.md#20-two-hashing-primitives-where-one-would-do) held that two hashing primitives where one would do is a fault, and its concrete worry was that the custom one "is the one every artifact identity depends on"; decision 0021 cleared it by deriving every stream from SHA-256 over a canonical record and deleting `Mix64`. Four facts about the code at `ef14630` decide how that argument applies here.

- **Identity already follows decision 0021 exactly.** An appearance's hash is the SHA-256 content hash of a canonical `surface-appearance/1` record written by `CanonicalWriter` ([decision 0020](0020-canonical-encoding-and-content-hash.md)), and the seed the image starts from is a reduction of that hash. No identity, no record name, and no stored byte rests on the mixer.
- **The mixer is not a stream derivation.** It is a spatial lattice hash *inside* one already-derived seed, and nothing downstream of it is stored or content-addressed: the image is reached only from the adapter at draw time and from the core's tests.
- **Decision 0021's own cost sentence is "That cost is per derived stream, not per draw."** An image is per draw. A 128-pixel-square texture of three-octave value noise costs of the order of 10^5 to 10^6 mixer calls, and at the 477 ns per SHA-256 that [review finding 20](../review.md#20-two-hashing-primitives-where-one-would-do) measured that is seconds for one body's face. Decision 0021's reasoning therefore supports a cheap construction here rather than merely tolerating one.
- **Portability is pinned the way this project pins it.** The arithmetic is integer throughout, and the suite records the literal digest `4a9c2dd16acc0ed321b19433bfdb4cd13e413371eeb11494ba0990e8c05bf2e8` for a 64-pixel-square image, which continuous integration compares on `ubuntu-latest` and `windows-latest`; a vector that holds literals is what [review finding 24](../review.md#24-tests-that-pin-nothing-the-recorded-vectors-do-not) asks of one.

## Decision

**The boundary.** [Decision 0021](0021-sha256-stream-derivation.md) governs stream derivation and identity-bearing hashing: wherever a random stream is derived, or a record, pack, or artifact is named by its content, the construction is SHA-256 over a canonical record and there is no second one. The internal arithmetic of a named rule is not that. A construction that consumes an already-derived seed, produces no stored and no content-addressed value, and runs per draw rather than per stream is admitted by being named, versioned, and frozen as one rule's implementation — never by being excepted from decision 0021, and never as a core primitive other code may reach for.

**`derive-surface-raster/1`.** The image is a named versioned rule in the manner of [decision 0037](0037-derivation-rules-integer-periods-and-orbit-hierarchy.md) and [decision 0049](0049-suit-profile-and-system-description-version-1-and-derivation-rules-as-named-implementations.md): typed inputs, one typed output, integer arithmetic throughout, and a recorded vector. Its inputs are one `surface-appearance/1` record and a side of 8 to 512 pixels; its output is `side × side` straight RGBA bytes in row order, the albedo image of that appearance. Version 1 is the implementation in `src/SpaceExplorer.Core/Appearance/SurfaceAppearance.cs` as of `ef14630` and is frozen there: changing the pattern set or any pattern's construction, the octave count or the lattice steps, the contrast curve, the seed reduction, or any constant below is `derive-surface-raster/2`, and nothing changes under version 1. As with the first two derivation rules, it is a named implementation before any category names it: no registry revision declares it, and its output is stored nowhere, so it is not a derived parameter in the sense of [decision 0037](0037-derivation-rules-integer-periods-and-orbit-hierarchy.md) and no manifest pins it ([decision 0049](0049-suit-profile-and-system-description-version-1-and-derivation-rules-as-named-implementations.md)).

**`surface-appearance/1` is a frozen canonical record** under [decision 0020](0020-canonical-encoding-and-content-hash.md): its domain label, and its fields in this order — shader, base colour, roughness and metallic as fractions of 65,535, pattern, scale in 1/65,536 m ([decision 0036](0036-frames-and-transforms-typed-by-connector-kind.md)), colour A, colour B, and contrast as a fraction of 65,535. It is read out of a stored `surface-material` and the `texture-recipe` it references and from nothing else, which is what makes a body's face content rather than a view's choice ([decision 0055](0055-definition-level-tags-and-tagged-references.md)). Its content hash is the appearance's identity, and the rule's seed is that hash's leading eight bytes read big-endian with the low bit set — no other seed is admitted, so two equal appearances give one image on every machine and two different ones cannot collide by accident. Every field therefore reaches the image through the seed; the pattern, the two colours, and the contrast additionally shape it, and the shader, base colour, roughness, metallic, and scale are what the engine adapter maps onto its material and its UV repeat.

**The mixer's constants belong to this rule.** The lattice position mix multiplies the x, y, and octave indices by `0x9E3779B97F4A7C15`, `0xC2B2AE3D27D4EB4F`, and `0x165667B19E3779F9` and folds them into the seed by exclusive or; the finalizer is MurmurHash3's 64-bit avalanche, the shifts 33, 29, and 32 around `0xFF51AFD7ED558CCD` and `0xC4CEB9FE1A85EC53`. They are frozen as version 1's implementation and are not a shared primitive: no other code may call them, and a second per-draw or per-sample construction takes a rule and a version of its own rather than reusing this one.

**The pixels are computed in the core.** [Architecture and ownership](../technical-design.md#architecture-and-ownership) separates the deterministic generator from the renderer and has worker tasks return plain data that the Godot adapter applies; a texture recipe is content and its image is data, so the core produces the bytes and the engine wraps them in a texture and a material. That position is what lets the vector above exist: an engine-side noise implementation could not be compared byte for byte on two operating systems by the core's suite, and the primitive-completeness invariant would then rest on an engine resource, which [decision 0031](0031-primitive-complete-composition-and-storage.md) forbids.

**An iteration row read from pictures pins the rule's version.** A verdict given on images is a verdict on this rule's output, so under [decision 0047](0047-usable-storage-and-structure-generation-then-solar-system-iterations.md) such a row pins `derive-surface-raster`'s version beside the registry revision, grammar version, generator, description version, and suit profile ([progress](../progress.md#solar-system-iterations)). Iteration 4 is the first and is read under version 1, the only version the build carries.

## Consequences

Easier: the format, the seed, the algorithm, and the layering of a body's face are now one record instead of a commit message, and the next author of a per-pixel or per-sample construction has a rule to apply rather than a precedent to argue. That is why this is written as a boundary and not as "the one exception to decision 0021": an exception invites the next construction to claim the same licence, while a boundary says what to do — keep identity and stream derivation on SHA-256, and name, version, and freeze the arithmetic inside a rule.

Harder: one more version to pin, and one whose drift is silent, because no hash moves when an image changes — only the recorded vector and the pictures a verdict was read from. Improving the noise is therefore a version increment that reissues a graphic sample, exactly as improving a description's wording is a description-version change ([decision 0049](0049-suit-profile-and-system-description-version-1-and-derivation-rules-as-named-implementations.md)). The recorded vector is this implementation's own digest, so unlike the published planetary figures behind `derive-surface-gravity/1` it freezes the image without independently proving it right; what makes it evidence of a portable image is continuous integration comparing it on both operating systems.

What must be verified: the recorded image vector on `ubuntu-latest` and `windows-latest`, under the Determinism check of the [development plan](../development-plan.md#verification-and-performance-targets); and, when the viewer of M0a exit criterion 11 is measured rather than reviewed, the cost of one image per body against the frame budget, which no measurement covers today. [Progress](../progress.md#m0a-exit-criteria) owns which of those hold. The adapter's caching and the lifetime of the textures it builds are its own concern and are not fixed here.
