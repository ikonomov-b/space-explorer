# 0048. Composition grammar version 1, the graph record, and graph publication

Date: 2026-09-08. Status: Accepted. Source: [decision 0047](0047-usable-storage-and-structure-generation-then-solar-system-iterations.md) step one, with the tag rule chosen by the owner on 2026-09-08 from the options in [review finding 43](../review.md#43-connector-tags-do-not-say-which-side-of-an-edge-provides-them). Implements the composition half of [decision 0031](0031-primitive-complete-composition-and-storage.md) as [decision 0042](0042-category-registry-revision-1-first-content-records-generator-and-publish-protocol.md) implemented the set half. Clears [review findings 43 to 45](../review.md#findings-raised-by-the-composition-generator).

## Context

Decision 0047 step one requires "a composition generator that builds a bounded, ordered graph of instances from a stored set under a versioned grammar, with instance paths, transforms typed by connector kind, one stream per instance, and connector-tag compatibility enforced, published and reloaded through the same protocol as sets". The documents fix what the pieces are: [decision 0031](0031-primitive-complete-composition-and-storage.md) gives the grammar its job, the instance path, and the bounds; [decision 0036](0036-frames-and-transforms-typed-by-connector-kind.md) fixes the three transform types and their fields; [decision 0035](0035-category-registry-record-and-generator-revision-identifiers.md) makes the registry a canonical record whose revision every dependent record's label carries. Three things they do not fix, all of which the first generator had to answer: which of a candidate child's connector declarations supplies the tags a parent requires ([finding 43](../review.md#43-connector-tags-do-not-say-which-side-of-an-edge-provides-them)), what a required child does when the depth or instance bound leaves no room for it ([finding 44](../review.md#44-a-required-child-and-the-depth-bound-can-contradict-each-other)), and where the bounds a transform's values are drawn from live ([finding 45](../review.md#45-a-transforms-fields-are-fixed-but-nothing-bounds-their-values)).

## Decision

**The grammar is a canonical record.** A composition grammar is one canonical record with domain label `composition-grammar/V/registry/N`, where `V` is the grammar version and `N` the category-registry revision it names categories and connectors of, identified exactly by its content hash. Version 1 is defined in code and frozen by its recorded hash, as registry revision 1 is; a second version or an authoring tool moves the source form to `content/`. It carries the depth, instance, and retry bounds, the weighted root categories per composition domain, and one production per reachable category, each giving per connector kind a child-count range inside the connector's own cardinality, the eligible child categories with integer weights, and the transform range. A grammar carries no dead rule: the productions are exactly the categories its roots reach. Every graph specification pins the version and the hash, so changing any count, weight, or range is a new grammar version, exactly as a parameter-schema change is a new registry revision.

**Transform ranges live in the grammar.** Decision 0036 fixes each transform type's components and their widths; the bounds a value is drawn between are numeric content, so they are declared per connector rule in the grammar and pinned by its hash, and revised by a grammar version rather than by a definition or a registry revision. This keeps measurement and tuning out of any hashed definition, as [decision 0034](0034-storage-policy-pinned-by-manifests.md) keeps the storage policy out of one.

**A connector label names a role.** A candidate child satisfies a parent's connector when its declaration for its own like-labelled connector kind provides every tag the parent's declaration for that connector requires. A category with no connector of that label provides nothing and so fits only where the parent requires no tag. Nothing else is compared: a definition's other connectors describe other roles.

**Instance paths and streams.** The root's path is its composition domain's label; a child's path is `<parent path>/<connector label>/<index>`, the index counting from zero in child order. A parent's stream draws only how many children each of its connector kinds takes; every instance's own stream, derived from the seed and its own path ([decision 0021](0021-sha256-stream-derivation.md)), draws its definition, its attachment transform, and its own child counts. No instance's content therefore depends on its siblings, and adding a field to a transform cannot shift a parent's structural draws.

**Bounds bind before an instance is placed.** A grammar states, from its own rules, the least depth and the least instance count each category costs with its required children, refusing at construction a category that requires itself through such a chain and a root whose minimum does not fit the bounds. An instance is placed only where its whole required structure still fits the depth and instance bounds: an optional child that does not fit is not placed, which is the structural absence an empty optional connector already means, and a required child that cannot be placed, for want of room or of a compatible definition, fails the run explicitly with the instance path and the reason. Nothing is substituted.

**The graph is one canonical record.** A composition graph is one canonical record with domain label `composition-graph/1/registry/N`, carrying its specification's fields inline so the graph reproduces from its own bytes, then the declared instance count, then the instances depth first, each as its definition's pack-local identifier, its transform, and a child count per connector kind. The pinned set supplies every definition hash, so an instance names a local identifier rather than an exact reference. The graph's pack identifier is the leading 16 bytes of its specification hash, as a generated set's is ([decision 0020](0020-canonical-encoding-and-content-hash.md)). Publication and reload use the set protocol of decision 0042: the set the graph composes must already be published with the manifest the graph pins, the record is written to a temporary file, flushed, re-read and verified, then moved into place, and the index row and its reference-index edges to the set and the grammar are committed last ([decision 0040](0040-reference-index-and-deletion-sweep.md)). One graph pack identifier holds one graph record; a differing one is a reproduction failure ([decision 0039](0039-one-manifest-per-pack-identifier.md)). A graph pinning an unsupported registry revision or another grammar is refused by name, never reinterpreted.

**Grammar version 1 over registry revision 1.** A solar system is rooted at exactly one star ([decision 0044](0044-tiered-system-composition-one-star-and-explorability-by-validation.md)), which takes one to eight bodies on its orbit connector, weighted nine to one towards a planet over a barycentre, and a barycentre takes exactly two planets. Registry revision 1 gives a planet exactly one atmosphere, so grammar version 1 gives every body one, an airless one where there is no air, as decision 0031 requires of meaningful absence. A moon is a planet on a planet's orbit connector, because revision 1 has no moon category, so the depth bound of three is what stops a moon of a moon; that model is one of the things the iterations of decision 0047 are meant to judge. An artifact is rooted at one part, which takes up to three attached parts, each within a quarter metre of its parent, so three attachment edges keep a part within 0.75 m of the root and inside the two-metre cap of [decision 0046](0046-artifact-families-human-height-cap-cargo-by-mass-and-material-rarity.md). Every count, weight, and range in it is untuned numeric content.

## Consequences

Easier: the first structures compose from stored sets with nothing but data, so decision 0047's iterations can begin; a graph is one verified record that reproduces from its own bytes; the grammar is the one place a count, a weight, or a transform range is tuned, and its hash makes a tuning change visible in every graph that pins it; a bound that cannot be met is an explicit named failure at the instance that could not be filled, never a silent substitution. Harder: a grammar version is now a third thing to keep supported beside the registry revision and the generator version, and retiring one is as deliberate; the required-structure rule means an authored grammar can be refused at construction for bounds that look reasonable in isolation; and the first grammar's moon model is knowingly wrong, so the first iterations will supersede it and every graph composed under version 1 with it. The authored `basic` vocabulary is corrected in the same change: its one artifact part required a tag it did not provide, so under any tag rule no part could attach to another, which composition made visible at once.

Verified by the Determinism and Primitive-set foundation checks in the [development plan](../development-plan.md#verification-and-performance-targets): a recorded fixed-seed graph hash per domain, run on Linux and Windows in continuous integration, and the two-process comparison over `compose`.
