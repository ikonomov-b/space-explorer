# 0062. The surface harness walks in an interactive session, and its two screenshot frames are unchanged

Date: 2026-09-11. Status: Accepted. Source: the owner's request of 2026-09-11, made while reading surface cycle one at the window, that he be able to walk the region he is judging rather than only look at two fixed frames; asked whether to add movement or to keep the frames exactly as clause 7 specifies, he chose movement, and he settled clauses 4 and 6 the same day, both on the recommended option. Adds interactive movement to the review harness of [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 7 and supersedes no part of it: the two frames that clause names, and the pictures a surface row cites, stand as written.

## Context

Clause 7 of [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) fixes the evidence a surface iteration is read from — a walk frame at 2 m eye height at the region's centre looking along the stored heading, and a top-down orthographic frame of the whole region with a scale bar, one pair per seed, each saved by `--screenshot` ([tools](../tools.md#godot)). It says what a picture is. It does not say the harness may not move while a person is sitting at it, and no other record does.

The request came out of cycle one's own reading. That cycle's frames answer periodicity at one standing point — on seed 1's body the material lays 5,891 tiles of 0.348 m, and at walking distance the grain reads as grain with no repeat visible ([progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations)). But a repeat is a property of the ground over distance, and a single pose can only report the pose it was taken at: it cannot distinguish ground that does not repeat from ground whose repeat falls outside one frustum. Walking is the cheapest way to ask rung one's own question across the whole 4.19 km² of a maximal region, on a view that already draws the ground at its true extent and lays the material at the length the recipe's scale claims.

Movement does not cross the line [decision 0045](0045-close-views-only-in-the-surface-sky-and-a-derived-system-description.md) draws. What that record forbids is a close view in the core release, and what [decision 0052](0052-the-two-levers-compose-a-destination-and-a-review-view-shows-it.md) settled, and clause 7 reaffirmed, is that a harness for reading a generator's output is not a core-release feature. Movement changes who may sit at the window, not what is shipped: there is still no approach scene, no arrival, no expedition, no preparation cost ([decision 0004](0004-preparation-is-real-generation-time.md)), and no hazard — nothing a player is given. A player-facing surface view would reverse that clause and is not proposed here either.

## Decision

Each clause stands alone. A clause marked **open** is a choice this record puts to the owner; a clause marked **follows** applies an accepted record.

**1. The walk frame moves in an interactive run (open; the owner chose movement on 2026-09-11).** Free horizontal movement over the ground at clause 7's fixed eye height, with left-drag mouse-look for yaw and pitch, `shift` for a faster pace: exactly the system view's conventions, which a reader of these two harnesses should have to learn once ([tools](../tools.md#godot)). There is no vertical movement and no flight. The eye stays at the 2 m that clause 7 takes from [decision 0041](0041-planet-fields-tangent-regions-minimum-radius-and-far-field.md)'s derivation of the minimum landable radius, so the pose a reader walks in and the pose the picture is taken at are the same pose, and a walk cannot report ground the frame could never show.

**2. The map frame does not move (follows).** `--from-above` keeps the fixed orthographic pose of clause 7. It frames the whole region by construction, so panning or turning it could only take out of frame the thing it exists to show, and an orthographic eye is not a person standing anywhere.

**3. The screenshot path is untouched (follows).** A run given `--screenshot` keeps the fixed pose and exits on its fourth frame, before a key could reach it, so a screenshot stays inherently a fixed frame. Nothing a surface row cites therefore moves: not the two frames of clause 7, not the sample kept at `build/samples/surface1-starter/` (clause 9), and not one of the pins of clause 10, whose vector is on the inputs. No graph, seed, hash, record, or picture changes, and cycle one's row reproduces as it stands ([progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations)).

**4. The walk is bounded by the region's own extent (open; the owner chose the bound on 2026-09-11).** The eye is held inside the region square, on the stored `extent` parameter the view already reads, so the bound is derived from the content rather than stated as a second constant ([decision 0017](0017-region-extent-cap-and-storage-derivation.md) caps that extent at 2,048 m). Beyond the ground there is nothing to see: the far field and the region edge are rung 5 and unbuilt, so a reader who walks off the plane is shown the absence of a later rung and not a property of what this one generates, and the boundary is the one [decision 0013](0013-host-simulates-hazards.md)'s tick enforces for real when there is a game to enforce it in.

**5. What the harness supplies, it says on screen (follows).** The pace, what `shift` multiplies it by, and the bound of clause 4 join the light direction, the sky colour, and the eye height in the legend, which is [decision 0057](0057-the-view-draws-what-the-content-stores.md)'s principle as clause 7 already applies it. The pace figures themselves are pinned by nothing and are named in no row, because the pictures a row cites are taken on clause 3's fixed pose; this record therefore states the two conditions they must meet rather than freezing numbers that carry no evidence — the grain must read at the pace a reader judges it at, and a maximal region must be crossable within a reading, which is what the two paces are for.

**6. `q` stops quitting (open; the owner chose to drop it on 2026-09-11).** `escape` quits, alone. `q` quits the surface view today ([tools](../tools.md#godot)) while the system view spends `q` and `e` on vertical movement, so a reader moving between the two harnesses would end his session by pressing a key that moves in the other one. `escape` means the same thing in both.

## Consequences

Easier: rung one's question is asked across the whole region instead of at one pose per seed, which is the reason the owner asked; the two harnesses share one set of controls, stated once in [tools](../tools.md#godot); and looking somewhere else costs a step rather than a re-render.

Harder: the harness carries input handling the screenshot path never exercises, which is scaffolding by [decision 0024](0024-tests-comments-index-and-scaffolding-trimmed.md)'s measure unless a person uses it — its whole warrant is the owner's reading, and if the reading stops using it, it should go. And a reader can now see a region from poses no picture records, so a fault found on foot has to be reported with where it was seen, where a fixed frame carried that for free. The verdict still rests on the pictures and on the pins of clause 10, and what a walk shows that a frame does not belongs in the row's prose beside the faults cycle one already names ([progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations)).

**What must be verified**, on the evidence [progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations) records: the walk frame saved by `--screenshot` opens at the same pose as before this record and still exits on its fourth frame, and `--from-above` is unchanged, which is clause 3's whole claim; the walk is held inside the stored extent; the legend names the pace and the bound; and the exported-build smoke check still prints `SMOKE OK`, which a review path must not disturb, as [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) requires of the same view. No vector, hash, or row moves, so a continuous-integration run evidences that the build and the suite are undisturbed and nothing more.
