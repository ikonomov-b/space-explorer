# 0047. Next steps: usable primitive storage and structure generation, then solar-system randomization in description-judged iterations

Date: 2026-09-08. Status: Accepted. Source: owner instruction of 2026-09-08. Reorders the immediate implementation steps that [progress](../progress.md#m0a-exit-criteria) listed after [decision 0042](0042-category-registry-revision-1-first-content-records-generator-and-publish-protocol.md) and extends the overlap [decision 0030](0030-m0a-criterion-1-proceeds-before-the-p0-exit-record.md) accepted between milestones.

## Context

After decision 0042 the immediate steps were, in order: the two-process determinism comparison, multi-revision loading, the content-gate test plan, the minimal Godot viewer, and the content gate. The owner instructs two next steps instead. First, the primitive storage and the generation of randomized structures from the primitives must reach a usable state. Second, the process that randomizes a solar system is crucial for all later randomization and for how primitives group, so it is planned next, as generating iterations whose criterion is the text description of each generated system, the description [decision 0045](0045-close-views-only-in-the-surface-sky-and-a-derived-system-description.md) placed at M0b. Registry revision 1 has `star`, `barycentre`, `planet`, and `atmosphere` but no life category, and the description's explorability flag is a validation outcome that needs regions ([decision 0044](0044-tiered-system-composition-one-star-and-explorability-by-validation.md)), which do not exist at the system level.

## Decision

**Step one: usable storage and structure generation.** Set generation, publication, reload, and validation already hold (M0a criterion 3). The step adds the two-process determinism comparison over `generate-set`; the loader accepting every registry revision this build supports, as [decision 0035](0035-category-registry-record-and-generator-revision-identifiers.md) requires, tested by loading a revision 1 pack beside a revision 2; and a composition generator that builds a bounded, ordered graph of instances from a stored set under a versioned grammar, with instance paths, transforms typed by connector kind ([decision 0036](0036-frames-and-transforms-typed-by-connector-kind.md)), one stream per instance, and connector-tag compatibility enforced, published and reloaded through the same protocol as sets and frozen by a recorded fixed-seed graph hash on both operating systems. Nothing else of M0a is a prerequisite for step two.

**Step two: the solar-system iterations.** M0b opens with the solar-system domain: a star root, planets on orbit connectors, moons, and atmospheres, with the derivation rules of [decision 0037](0037-derivation-rules-integer-periods-and-orbit-hierarchy.md) the description needs. The describe command of decision 0045 is the first deliverable, because it is the criterion. A generation iteration is a numbered round that pins its registry revision and hash, vocabulary hash, grammar version, generator version, and a fixed seed list per tier, generates one system per seed, and prints every description. The validator checks each description against its tier's rules of decision 0044 mechanically; the owner reads the whole sample and records a verdict, plausible and varied or not, together with what the next iteration changes. Every iteration is recorded in [progress](../progress.md#solar-system-iterations) and reproduces from its record. Iterations may revise the registry, for life, moons, or any category the descriptions show missing, and the vocabulary; each pins its own. Systems generated in iterations are disposable and are never kept destinations.

**The description at the system level.** Until regions exist, a planet is described as a landing candidate when its reference radius is at least 524,288 m ([decision 0041](0041-planet-fields-tangent-regions-minimum-radius-and-far-field.md)) and its derived surface gravity, surface pressure, and equilibrium temperature fall inside the suit profile's envelope; the validation outcome of decision 0044 replaces that flag once regions are generated, a version change of the description under decision 0045. Life-bearing follows the presence of a life primitive, so the second tier's life rule is checkable only from the iteration whose registry revision adds a life category; earlier iterations are judged on the starter tier and on the structural rules alone.

**Order of the rest.** The content-gate test plan of [decision 0002](0002-primitive-set-content-gate.md), the minimal Godot viewer, and the content gate follow step two. Requirement R6 holds throughout: every structure composes from stored sets, and no world generation substitutes for the primitive-set foundation. The compatibility cost of decision 0030 extends to this overlap: every set and system generated before the gate may be superseded. M0a's remaining criteria still close before M0b exits. P0 sessions remain required in parallel.

## Consequences

Easier: the generator's output is judged in text before any rendering exists, so the domain every other domain hangs from settles first, and the grouping of primitives, which categories compose under which connectors, is decided where it is cheapest to change; the describe command doubles as the M0b determinism vector; each iteration is a reproducible record rather than a conversation. Harder: the content gate moves later, so the first vocabularies are judged by description rather than silhouette and may be superseded; M0a stays in progress longer while its viewer and gate wait; the description carries a provisional landing-candidate flag whose wording changes when validation replaces it. Verified by the iteration records in progress and by the Destination validity check in the [development plan](../development-plan.md#verification-and-performance-targets), whose reviewed description sample begins with the iterations.
