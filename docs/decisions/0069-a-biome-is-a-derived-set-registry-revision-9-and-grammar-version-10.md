# 0069. A biome is a derived set: the `biome-element` category, `biome-patches/1`, the ocean datum, registry revision 9 and grammar version 10

Date: 2026-09-11. Status: Proposed. Source: the owner's ruling of 2026-09-11, "re-derive the budget upward, the derived set is the way", given on the [storage inspection](../inspection-storage.md)'s measurement and recorded from the budget side by [decision 0068](0068-campaign-storage-budget-re-derived-from-the-payload-ceiling.md), whose clause 4 leaves the per-region figure and the per-node bound to this record. This is rung 3 of the ladder [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 2 names, and it carries the registry revision and grammar version that rung needs in the same record, on the precedent of [decision 0055](0055-definition-level-tags-and-tagged-references.md) and of decisions 0061, 0063 and 0065. Nothing here is implemented.

## Context

Rung 2 is closed twice over. Relief is a per-planet recipe, `terrain-heightfield/1` under registry revision 6 ([decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md)); its shape was wrong for rock and `terrain-heightfield/2` folds it into ridges by a stored `relief-ridging` under revision 7 ([decision 0065](0065-terrain-heightfield-version-2-ridges-and-registry-revision-7s-ridging.md)); the ground is then written to the database as `region-payload/1` under revision 8 and the view only reads it ([decision 0066](0066-generation-writes-to-the-database-and-the-view-only-reads.md)), produced for every region when a destination is composed ([decision 0067](0067-a-destinations-ground-is-generated-when-it-is-composed.md)). All three cycles are accepted ([progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations)).

Rung 3's row names what it still needs: "The `biome element` category, and whether a biome is a field or a derived set". [Decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md)'s deferral table adds two more items to the same rung: "the biome index per cell that completes [decision 0017](0017-region-extent-cap-and-storage-derivation.md)'s 3 MiB, and the ocean datum [decision 0041](0041-planet-fields-tangent-regions-minimum-radius-and-far-field.md) names beside relief". This record answers all four.

**The field reading was considered and a measurement decided against it**, which is worth a reader's minute because [decision 0017](0017-region-extent-cap-and-storage-derivation.md) budgeted for it: that record's 3 MiB raw per region is 2 MiB of heights plus 1 MiB of a per-cell biome index, so the field reading — a per-planet recipe evaluated per cell, stored as a plane beside the heights — had a constant fitted to it since 2026-09-07. The [storage inspection](../inspection-storage.md#since-the-reading) measured what `region-payload/1` actually writes: heights alone pack to 0.35 to 1.26 MiB, mean 0.97, so heights alone sit at decision 0017's target for heights **and** biome together. A stored per-cell plane would then have cost a further 25 KB to 200 KB a region depending on how blotchy the content looked, which couples the storage budget to an appearance the vocabulary is retuned against every cycle. The owner ruled for the derived set and for re-deriving the budget upward; [decision 0068](0068-campaign-storage-budget-re-derived-from-the-payload-ceiling.md) carries the budget half and voids decision 0017's biome-map clause and its 4 m fallback, there being no map.

**What is actually in the build, checked rather than assumed.** [Decision 0026](0026-biome-rule-sets-and-recovery-challenges-are-template-vocabularies.md) says a biome rule set is a template vocabulary "scoped to the `biome element` category already in the primitive registry's `category` field". No registry revision of this build declares any such category: revision 8's are `star`, `barycentre`, `planet`, `atmosphere`, `surface-material`, `texture-recipe`, `geometry-recipe`, `artifact-part`, `artifact`, and `region`, and the word "biome" occurs in `src/` twice, both in doc comments saying the layer arrives with the rung that needs it. Nor does the [primitive registry](../technical-design.md#primitive-registry-and-composition)'s `category` row list category names today. [Decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md)'s deferral table is right — "which no built revision declares" — and decision 0026 stands as a definition whose premise about the registry was never true of code. This record is where the category arrives, and it makes decision 0026 implementable rather than superseding it: a biome rule set becomes a vocabulary of `biome-element` templates, which is what that record always meant.

**One anticipation this rung does not meet.** [Decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md) clause 6 reserves half of the int16 height range as "deliberate headroom for the biome-modulated relief of rung 3 and the far field of rung 5". Rung 3 as recorded here modulates no relief: a biome decides what the ground is made of and not how high it is, which is what [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 2's row asks for — "material variation across the region" — and one capability is what a rung adds. The headroom stays unspent, and clause 12 names where it goes.

## Decision

Each clause below stands alone, so the owner can accept or amend one at a time. A clause marked **the owner's ruling** records a choice already made and is not reopened here; a clause marked **open** is a choice this record puts to him, with the recommendation named; a clause marked **follows** applies an accepted record and is open only in the sense that anything is.

**1. A biome is a derived set (the owner's ruling of 2026-09-11).** A region node derives a bounded ordered set of **biome patches** under [decision 0038](0038-derived-instances.md): produced by the region node's generator revision identifier from the node's parameters and the node's own random stream alone, in deterministic order, addressed by extending the node's path with `item/<index>` — `solar-system/planet/N/region/0/item/K` — carrying parameters and a rigid transform in the region's frame, and never children or connectors. They are not graph nodes, allocate no ID, and count against the region's derived budget rather than against the graph's node limit.

The per-cell answer is not a third object and is not stored: it is derived from the set on load by the named rule of clause 5, exactly as a texture recipe's image is derived by `derive-surface-raster` and stored nowhere ([decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md)).

**2. `biome-element`, category 11 of registry revision 9 (open as to its parameters; recommended).** A leaf category with no connectors, admitted to the solar-system and surface domains as `region` is, whose generated definitions are what a patch is an instance of and whose authored templates are decision 0026's **biome rule set**. Its parameters, each carrying its unit, its default and the decoder's bounds inside the record as [decision 0060](0060-registry-revision-4-units-defaults-and-validation-limits.md) requires and [finding 50](../review.md#50-a-stored-quantity-carries-no-unit-no-default-and-no-validation-limit) set as the standard:

| Parameter | Kind | Unit | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `surface` | reference to `surface-material` | — | — | none, as every reference ([decision 0060](0060-registry-revision-4-units-defaults-and-validation-limits.md)) |
| `biome-submerged` | bool | — | — | false |
| `biome-elevation-low` | integer | ratio of 65,535 | −65,535 to 65,535 | −65,535 |
| `biome-elevation-high` | integer | ratio of 65,535 | −65,535 to 65,535 | 65,535 |

The elevation band is a signed fraction of the body's own `relief-amplitude` and not a metre length, so one authored biome suits a body of 90 m of relief and one of 1,024 m without being re-authored per body; the bound is ±65,535 because [decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md) clause 6 makes every height satisfy `|height| ≤ relief-amplitude`, so a band of ±1 covers every ground a body can make. The defaults are the widest band and dry land, so a template that ranges nothing authors a biome that claims anywhere — the same rule [decision 0065](0065-terrain-heightfield-version-2-ridges-and-registry-revision-7s-ridging.md) clause 1 applied to `relief-ridging`.

**Slope is deliberately absent**, though it is the classic third claim and the one that would paint a scarp differently from the bench below it. It needs a second derivative of the field at every cell and a second threshold to tune, and rung 3's verdict is whether material varies legibly and plausibly, which a submerged flag and an elevation band already answer. Adding it later is a parameter in a registry revision and a rule version, which is what the ladder costs at every rung anyway ([finding 54](../review.md#54-the-registry-the-grammars-and-the-numeric-tables-are-code-where-the-vocabulary-is-content)). This is [decision 0024](0024-tests-comments-index-and-scaffolding-trimmed.md)'s rule applied to a schema.

**3. The palette is the planet's, and reaches the region as parent-to-child data (open; recommended).** The `planet` category gains one parameter, `biomes`: an ordered array of 2 to 6 exact references to `biome-element` definitions, whose template range may demand tags of what it names exactly as `surface` already does ([decision 0055](0055-definition-level-tags-and-tagged-references.md)), so the `basic` vocabulary's rocky body draws `for-rocky` biomes and its ocean body `for-ocean` ones with no new region template and no new grammar demand. A patch names its biome by an ordinal into that array.

It sits on the planet and not on the region for the reason [decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md) clause 3 put the relief field there: a planet has one climate, and [decision 0041](0041-planet-fields-tangent-regions-minimum-radius-and-far-field.md) already says every region of one planet shares one relief, one climate and one sea level. Two regions of one body therefore draw from one palette even though clause 8 makes their patches independent. The palette reaches the region as parent-to-child **data** flow, which decision 0041 admits and which [decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md) already built for relief; the region's *stream* stays its own, which is the distinction [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 14 says is easy to state wrongly — the parent's path is inherited, the parent's stream is not.

The planet's own `surface` reference stays and is not replaced. It is what a body wears at system scale in the review view of [decision 0057](0057-the-view-draws-what-the-content-stores.md), where a body is a few pixels across; the palette is what the ground wears underfoot. A vocabulary may point both at one material and nothing breaks.

**4. The ordered-array parameter kind arrives with its first consumer (open; recommended).** Registry revision 9 adds `RefList` to the closed parameter schema: an ordered array of exact references to one category, with a declared minimum and maximum length checked before allocation. The [documented schema](../technical-design.md#primitive-registry-and-composition) has admitted ordered arrays since before revision 1 and no category has needed one, so this is the rule `ParameterKind`'s own remark states and [decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md) clause 1 restates — the kind arrives with the first category that stores one, and not before.

The alternative, four or six separately labelled reference parameters, was rejected: it fixes the arity in the registry where the vocabulary should choose it, it makes `biomes-3` a name a reader must decode, and it would have to be undone by a revision the first time a body wanted a fifth biome.

**5. Two named rules, because two different things happen (open as to the constructions; recommended).**

**`biome-patches/1`** is a generator revision identifier in [decision 0035](0035-category-registry-record-and-generator-revision-identifiers.md)'s `<generator>/<k>` form, listed by the `region` category, and it produces what is stored. From the region node's own stream ([decision 0021](0021-sha256-stream-derivation.md) over the instance path) it derives `k` patches in index order, where **`k = (extent / 256)²`** — 64 at the 2,048 m cap of [decision 0017](0017-region-extent-cap-and-storage-derivation.md) and 1 at the 256 m minimum extent registry revision 5 declares. Each patch draws, in this order: a palette ordinal uniform over the planet's `biomes` length; a centre `(x, z)` uniform over the region's extent in the 1/256 m units of [decision 0010](0010-units-coordinates-and-region-bounds.md); and a **reach** uniform over 128 to 512 m. Every draw is the bounded sampling [decision 0019](0019-generator-version-1-frozen.md) froze, so no new construction is introduced and no new anchor is owed.

Both constants are derived and not chosen. `k` gives a mean patch spacing of `extent / (extent / 256) = 256 m` at every extent, which is 128 cells at [decision 0010](0010-units-coordinates-and-region-bounds.md)'s 2 m cell, so no patch is smaller than a walker crossing it at the 2 m/s of [decision 0017](0017-region-extent-cap-and-storage-derivation.md) reads in about two minutes; and the reach range is half that spacing to twice it, so patches neither tile without overlap nor drown each other.

**`derive-biome-index/1`** is a derivation rule in the shape [decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md) fixes for `derive-surface-raster`: integer arithmetic throughout, no floating-point value admitted anywhere, no random stream, no draw, a frozen implementation, and recorded vectors. It takes the stored heights, the stored patch set, the planet's `sea-level` and the planet's palette, and returns one byte per **cell** — `1,024 × 1,024` for a maximal region, which is the per-cell biome index [decision 0017](0017-region-extent-cap-and-storage-derivation.md) named, now derived rather than stored. Per cell, at the cell's centre:

- the cell's height is the mean of the four lattice corners that bound it, which is why a height belongs to a corner and an index to a cell ([decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md) clause 6);
- **if that height is below the planet's `sea-level`**, the cell takes the lowest-numbered palette entry whose `biome-submerged` is true, and falls through to the next step where the palette holds none;
- otherwise the cell takes the patch of least `d² × 65,536 / r²` among the patches whose biome element admits the cell's elevation — `biome-elevation-low × amplitude ≤ height × 65,535 ≤ biome-elevation-high × amplitude`, in integers with no division — and among all patches where none admits it. Ties go to the lower patch index.

That the fallbacks exist is the point: the rule is **total**, so no cell and no region can fail, and clause 13 keeps [decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md) clause 15's deferral alive.

**6. The ocean datum is one number on the planet (open as to the constant; recommended).** The `planet` category gains `sea-level`: an integer in metres, the height of the sea surface above the body's reference sphere, range **−1,024 to 1,024 m**, default **−1,024 m**. A cell is submerged where its height is strictly below it.

Both ends are derived. The range is [decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md) clause 6's amplitude bound, `|height| ≤ relief-amplitude ≤ 1,024 m`, so a datum outside it could only mean all sea or no sea, which the ends already mean. The default is the floor because no height is strictly below the floor, so a body that declares nothing has no sea — a derived sentinel rather than a chosen one, and the rule [decision 0065](0065-terrain-heightfield-version-2-ridges-and-registry-revision-7s-ridging.md) clause 1 set: a template that ranges nothing keeps the ground it had.

Water is therefore a biome and not a second mechanism: a palette entry whose `biome-submerged` is true, wearing its own `surface-material`. This is what the ocean body of cycle three needs. Its whole sampled region lies below the reference radius at −22.8 m to −8.9 m with 13.9 m of relief across 2,048 m, and it "reads as a sea, and it reads that way for the wrong reason — there is no water, only flat ground wearing a blue material" ([progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations)). With a datum authored inside that range the same body gets a shoreline, which is a thing a reader can judge, and the reading stops being right by accident.

**A named error, not a fixed one.** The datum is a sphere of radius `R + sea-level` and a region is a plane tangent at `R`, so a flat water surface drawn at a constant region-local height is up to **2.0 m** too high at a maximal region's corner on a body of the minimum landable radius. That is [decision 0041](0041-planet-fields-tangent-regions-minimum-radius-and-far-field.md)'s already-accepted flat-model error, the same 2.0 m that record derives for the ground itself, and it is not paid for again here; whether rung 5 buys an exact datum with the integer square root [decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md) defers for the seam is one question and not two.

**7. `region-payload/2` carries the derived set (open; recommended).** The payload grows a version rather than splitting into a second record, on [decision 0050](0050-registry-revision-2-grammar-version-2-and-versioned-record-growth.md)'s rule that a version's added field is written only from that version on: version 1's bytes, its recorded hash, and surface cycles one to three are unmoved and reproduce. Version 2 is version 1's fields — the region's instance path, the heights' rule, the `relief-field` hash, the extent, and the heights — followed by the patch rule's identifier and the ordered patch array, each patch being its palette ordinal, its centre `(x, z)` in 1/256 m, and its reach in metres.

One record and not two, for a reason the format already enforces once: [decision 0066](0066-generation-writes-to-the-database-and-the-view-only-reads.md) makes a destination complete or absent so that no stored destination is missing its ground, and two records per region would reintroduce exactly that hole one level down — a region whose heights exist and whose biomes do not, which nothing would refuse. [Decision 0038](0038-derived-instances.md) says the same thing from the other side: under `materialize` or `hybrid` "the accepted derived set is **the node's** primitive-owned payload", singular, and the [persistence table](../technical-design.md#persistence-and-compatibility) already lists it as a bounded ordered array whose length is checked against the declared maximum before allocation.

**8. What this layer costs, under decision 0068's ceiling (follows; the figure [decision 0068](0068-campaign-storage-budget-re-derived-from-the-payload-ceiling.md) clause 4 asks this record for).** A patch is a palette ordinal in one byte, two centre coordinates whose zig-zagged range of ±262,144 units needs three varint bytes each, and a reach in two: **nine bytes**. The region's derived budget is 64, so the array is at most 576 B, and **under 640 B** with its count and the patch rule's identifier. Against decision 0068 clause 1's ceiling of 2,234,560 B for a maximal region's payload that is **0.03%**, and against its measured mean of 0.97 MiB it is 0.06%. The 640 MiB campaign budget of that record's clause 3 is unmoved by this rung, and a destination's mean 4.6 regions gain under 3 KB.

Deriving the index on load is bounded by the same arithmetic: at most 64 patches tested over 1,048,576 cells is 67.1 million integer comparisons, against the order of 10^8 lattice evaluations a heightfield costs, measured at 53 ms warm and 322 ms on a cold process's first call ([progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations)). The classification is therefore tens of milliseconds, derived from a measured figure rather than guessed, and clause 14 records the real one.

**9. Deriving on load does not reverse decision 0066, and the line that says so is worth stating (follows).** [Decision 0066](0066-generation-writes-to-the-database-and-the-view-only-reads.md) clause 1 says the view "calls no generation rule", for two reasons: a renderer that generates is a second implementation of the world that can draw what is not stored, and a stored surface is stable against a change to its own rule. `derive-biome-index/1` breaches neither. It takes **no random stream and no draw**, so it cannot produce anything the store does not already contain — it is a pure function of stored bytes, which is a projection and not a generation, and the distinction is one the documents already live by: `derive-surface-raster` turns a stored recipe into pixels on every run and no image is stored anywhere ([decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md), [decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md) clause 10). The rule runs in Core and the engine only wraps its output, as [decision 0057](0057-the-view-draws-what-the-content-stores.md) requires.

The second reason is met in the half that matters and conceded in the half that does not. What carries identity — the patches, and the palette entries they name — is stored, so a revisit finds the same biomes in the same places and rung 4 has stable paths to hang on. What a `derive-biome-index/2` could move is where one biome gives way to the next, which is the same exposure `derive-surface-raster/2` already took when it redrew every body's material ([decision 0064](0064-surface-raster-version-2-tiles-seamlessly-and-finely.md)) and which the documents accepted then. It is named here rather than discovered later.

**10. Registry revision 9 and grammar version 10 (open as to their content; follows as to their form).** Revision 9 is revision 8 with:

- the `RefList` parameter kind of clause 4;
- the new `biome-element` category of clause 2;
- `planet` gaining `biomes` (clause 3) and `sea-level` (clause 6);
- `region` gaining a derived-instance budget of **64** and `biome-patches/1` in its generator revision identifiers beside `terrain-heightfield/1` and `/2`. Its permitted policies stay revision 8's `Regenerate | Materialize`, which is what [decision 0038](0038-derived-instances.md) requires before a derived set may be a payload at all.

Grammar version 10 lays version 9's rules over revision 9 and **adds no production**, exactly as versions 7, 8 and 9 stood to the versions before them: a planet still carries one region wherever `admit-landable-body/1` admits one, and a palette is a parameter rather than a child. Revisions 1 to 8 and grammar versions 1 to 9 stay supported, so every surface cycle and every solar-system iteration reproduces from its record ([decision 0050](0050-registry-revision-2-grammar-version-2-and-versioned-record-growth.md)). The vocabulary is authored for the newest revision, so its hash moves and re-mints the set pack and every destination; that is no longer free, and [decision 0067](0067-a-destinations-ground-is-generated-when-it-is-composed.md) clause 5 is why — a re-mint now costs the ground of every destination as well as the set.

**11. The view draws the biomes and the sea, and says what it supplied (open as to the frames; follows as to the principle).** The ground mesh `SurfaceView` already builds from the heightfield gains a per-vertex biome material: the index plane selects among the palette's materials, each rastered by `derive-surface-raster/2` ([decision 0064](0064-surface-raster-version-2-tiles-seamlessly-and-finely.md)) at its own stored scale. A flat plane is drawn at `sea-level` in the region's frame wearing the submerged biome's material, where the palette holds one — opaque, no transparency, no waves, no shore foam and no refraction, all of which are appearance nobody has asked for and which [decision 0002](0002-primitive-set-content-gate.md) and [decision 0024](0024-tests-comments-index-and-scaffolding-trimmed.md) refuse to scaffold.

The legend gains the palette — each biome's label, its material and its claim — and the sea level as stored lines, and names three things as supplied: how one material is blended into the next at a boundary, the water plane's flatness with clause 6's 2.0 m stated, and whatever the mesh does to carry more than one material, which is the engine's business and not the content's. That is [decision 0057](0057-the-view-draws-what-the-content-stores.md)'s principle as [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 7 and [decision 0062](0062-the-surface-harness-walks-in-an-interactive-session.md) clause 5 already apply it.

**12. The sweep draws a region per kind, not the first of each destination (open; recommended; this is the owner's to settle).** Cycle three's reading found a gap in the harness and not in the content: a walk frame is drawn of the destination's **first** region, and the first region of all twenty-four seeds sits on a rocky body, so forty-eight frames showed forty-eight rocky grounds — for the cycle whose whole change was that shape depends on what a body is made of. Of the sample's 120 regions, 104 are rock, 10 ocean and 6 ice, and six frames had to be rendered by name to read the change at all ([progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations)).

Rung 3 makes this worse, because a biome layer varies by what it sits on and the ocean datum is read on ocean bodies alone. The recommendation is the cheapest thing that fixes it: **the seed's index selects the body kind, and the sweep draws that seed's first region of that kind, falling back to its first region where the kind is absent.** The sample stays forty-eight frames and costs the owner's reading nothing extra; the selection is a deterministic function of the seed and the graph, so it reproduces and cycle four compares with cycle three frame for frame; and the row prints the kind beside each frame, so what was read is on the record.

Two alternatives, named so the choice is one: a frame per kind per seed is six frames a seed and 144 in all, which triples the reading for a coverage the rotation already gives; and leaving the sweep alone keeps the fault, which the next three rungs — biome, scatter, sites — each make worse than the last.

**13. Rung 3 does not make the surface fallible (follows).** [Decision 0063](0063-relief-by-terrain-heightfield-registry-revision-6-and-grammar-version-7.md) clause 15 defers the per-phase retry and refund rule to "rung 3 or 4". It is not rung 3: `biome-patches/1` draws from a palette the generator has already filled and `derive-biome-index/1` is total by clause 5's two fallbacks, so no region can fail and nothing retries. The deferral stands, to rung 4, where a site that will not fit is the first thing on this ladder that can refuse. The landing-candidate flag and description version 3 are likewise untouched, for the reason [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 12 gives: a region still cannot pass or fail the suit profile.

**14. What proves it (follows, on the precedents of decisions 0020, 0056, 0063 and 0065).** Four vectors, no two of which rest on the same thing.

- **A hand case.** A named `region-payload/2` of a small extent — 256 m, so 129 × 129 heights, 128 × 128 cells and one patch — with a named palette and a named `sea-level`, whose full classification is written out in the suite and computed by an implementation outside this repository from this record's prose alone, as both of `terrain-heightfield/1`'s were.
- **An inherited anchor, and no new one owed.** Neither rule introduces a hash or a mixer construction: the patches are drawn through [decision 0019](0019-generator-version-1-frozen.md)'s bounded sampling over [decision 0021](0021-sha256-stream-derivation.md)'s stream derivation, both already anchored on PCG32's published demo and SHA-256's published vectors, and the classification is comparison and integer multiplication. [Decision 0056](0056-surface-raster-rule-and-the-boundary-of-stream-derivation.md)'s prohibition on a second hash construction is therefore not engaged, which is worth recording because every rule on this ladder so far had to answer it.
- **A portability digest.** The SHA-256 of a full 1,024 × 1,024 index plane for one named payload and one named palette, compared on `ubuntu-latest` and `windows-latest`, as `terrain-heightfield`'s field digest already is.
- **Two properties.** Every cell below `sea-level` takes a submerged biome wherever the palette holds one, which is clause 6's whole claim stated as a test; and every index is inside the palette and every cell carries one, which is clause 5's totality and what clause 13 rests on.

Mutation-checked, as every rule here is: classifying by unweighted distance rather than by `d² / r²`, letting a cell below the datum take a dry biome, deriving the patch set from the planet's stream rather than the region's, and ignoring a biome element's elevation band should each turn the suite red.

**15. The sample, the counts, and the pins (follows).** The full twenty-four starter lever seeds, as [decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md) clause 8 fixed from rung 2 on, drawn under clause 12's selection. Beside the owner's verdict the row prints the counts decisions 0061 clause 11 and 0063 clause 12 already ask for — regions per destination, distinct surface materials, distinct relief fields — and two of the same cheap kind for what this rung adds: **distinct biome elements across the sample**, and **biomes actually present per region**, the second because a palette of six that paints one is a fault a picture might not show. That is [finding 55](../review.md#55-uniqueness-is-unguarded-above-the-artifact)'s recommended step: make repetition visible rather than corrected. The pins are decision 0061 clause 10's with `biome-patches` and `derive-biome-index` added beside `terrain-heightfield` and `derive-surface-raster`, because a verdict read from pictures is a verdict on every rule whose output is in them.

## Consequences

Easier: the one question rung 3's row left open is settled, and it is settled on a measurement rather than on a preference — the derived set adds 0.03% of a payload's ceiling where a stored plane would have added 1% to 9% of it, coupled to how blotchy the content looked; decision 0026's biome rule set becomes a thing that can be authored, four decisions and two months after it was defined; the ocean body stops being right by accident; a biome carries identity and a path, so rung 4 has something to hang scatter and a site on; and `biome-element` puts a content judgement — what a world is made of, and where — in the authored vocabulary, which is the half of [finding 54](../review.md#54-the-registry-the-grammars-and-the-numeric-tables-are-code-where-the-vocabulary-is-content)'s cost that a rung can repay while it is adding to the other half.

Harder: a ninth registry revision and a tenth grammar version join the eight and nine retained, and every command still selects by what the content pins; a parameter kind joins the closed schema, with a writer, a reader, a bound and a generator path of its own; the payload grows to a second version whose first version is retained for good, because cycles one to three cite it; two more rules are retained implementations for as long as any supported revision lists them ([decision 0035](0035-category-registry-record-and-generator-revision-identifiers.md)); and re-minting the set now re-mints every destination's ground with it, which [decision 0067](0067-a-destinations-ground-is-generated-when-it-is-composed.md) clause 5 priced at megabytes rather than kilobytes.

**A known cost, chosen a fourth time.** [Decision 0061](0061-surface-iterations-by-an-escalating-ladder-registry-revision-5-and-grammar-version-6.md)'s Consequences counted four rungs that would each fire [finding 54](../review.md#54-the-registry-the-grammars-and-the-numeric-tables-are-code-where-the-vocabulary-is-content)'s trigger; this is the third of those rungs and the fifth registry revision to fire it, rung 2 alone having taken three. Finding 54 stays **open** and this record clears nothing of it. What it does do is shift the balance: the palette, every biome's claim, and the sea level are content, so what the owner is most likely to want to tune after reading the sample — which materials, where, and how much sea — is a file edit and a rerun, and only the category, the rules and the grammar are a rebuild.

**What must be verified**, on the evidence [progress](../progress.md#surface-iterations) records: the four vectors of clause 14 and the four mutations beside them; registry revision 9's and grammar version 10's frozen hashes beside the earlier ones, with every earlier revision's and version's hash unmoved, so cycles one to three and every solar-system iteration still reproduce; a `region-payload/2` published and reloaded byte for byte, and a version 1 payload still loading beside it; two processes deriving the same patch set for one region byte for byte, as [decision 0048](0048-composition-grammar-version-1-graph-records-and-graph-publication.md)'s graphs already are; a patch array longer than the declared budget refused before allocation; a `biomes` array outside its 2-to-6 bound and a `sea-level` outside clause 6's range each refused by name; the classification cost of a maximal region measured beside the heightfield's 53 ms; the per-region byte figure of clause 8 measured against decision 0068's ceiling; the description's version 3 vector unmoved, which is clause 13's claim; and the exported-build smoke check still printing `SMOKE OK`, which a review path must not disturb. The Primitive-set foundation, Determinism and Resource-use checks of the [development plan](../development-plan.md#verification-and-performance-targets) own those; Surface-sky consistency stays at Not started until rung 6.
